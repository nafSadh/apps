Arsenal agree Konsa deal; Liverpool's Jones set for Inter move

Arsenal have agreed a deal worth more than £50m for Aston Villa defender Ezri Konsa. BBC Sport reports a medical is to follow, and Transfermarkt separately confirms the agreement; both tie the signing to cover for the injured William Saliba. Each describes it as agreed rather than officially completed. Sky Sports, from a different newsroom, quotes Mikel Arteta as "100 per cent" confident Myles Lewis-Skelly will stay at the club this window.

Liverpool's Curtis Jones is reported to be leaving for Inter Milan. Sky Sports says the clubs have agreed a £30m fee, and Transfermarkt and FourFourTwo separately cover the same move, the last questioning a sale that it says leaves Andoni Iraola's Liverpool "one injury from a full-blown crisis." Neither club has announced it, so it stays reported rather than done. Transfermarkt also lists Adam Wharton as a target, while Sky reports Crystal Palace expect the midfielder to stay.

At Manchester City, Sky Sports and BBC Sport — two independent newsrooms — both report Tottenham are in talks to sign Savinho and Omar Marmoush, and BBC's gossip column has City joining Manchester United in the race for Roma's Manu Kone. Transfermarkt reports Tijjani Reijnders's move to the Saudi Pro League as complete. In France, the Guardian says PSG's Ligue 1 opener against Rennes has been switched from the Parc des Princes after heat damaged the pitch.
