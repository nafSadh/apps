Guimaraes fee reported agreed; Real Madrid weigh Vinicius exit

The clearest of Saturday's transfer stories: BBC's gossip column reported Arsenal and Newcastle have agreed a fee for Bruno Guimaraes, a line also carried by BBC Sport — though both are BBC properties, so that's the same newsroom's report distributed twice, not independent confirmation. The same column said Inter Milan have increased their bid for Liverpool's Curtis Jones, building on interest first reported on July 29. Neither deal has been confirmed by the clubs involved.

Arsenal's interest in Real Madrid's Vinicius Jr, first reported by BBC on July 25, resurfaced Saturday when Sky's papers round-up said Real Madrid are now open to letting him leave — a claim also carried by Sky Sports, again the same outlet's own reporting rather than a second source. That directly conflicts with BBC's July 29 gossip column, which said Real Madrid had no plans to sell him. The feed doesn't resolve which read is current.
