Vinícius banned four games as PSG rout Man City

Real Madrid's Vinícius Júnior has been handed a four-match ban following his red card against Villarreal — confirmed and already in effect. Both the Guardian and BBC Sport, genuinely separate newsrooms rather than two outlets under one roof, reported the suspension and carried his follow-up comments that he is unfairly targeted by referees and that flair players need more protection. That is Vinícius's own characterization of his treatment, not an established fact, but the ban itself is settled, and the two-outlet sourcing makes this the best-corroborated story of the day.

Champions League results also moved things for two of the six clubs tracked here. PSG beat Manchester City away at the Etihad — City's first home game since the Premier League's guilty verdict — with the Guardian covering the match from several angles, including a description of the hosts spinning into a 'dizzy mess.' Real Madrid needed a rally of their own: per Opta Analyst, they trailed at Roma before Antonio Rüdiger completed a seven-minute comeback for a 4-3 win.

Elsewhere, Bayern Munich and Harry Kane have agreed a new contract in principle through 2029, per Sky Sports — reported by a single outlet, not yet a confirmed signing. Liverpool's news was less positive: the Guardian reports Giovanni Leoni's comeback from the ACL injury sustained on his debut is taking longer than expected, per manager Andoni Iraola. FourFourTwo separately reported, as an unconfirmed single-source claim, that a major January Liverpool signing has been 'given the green light,' with Florian Wirtz potentially making way.
