Man City reject second Barcelona bid for Rodri

Manchester City have rejected a second Barcelona bid for Rodri, according to both Sky Sports and the Guardian, reporting independently. The two disagree on the fee: Sky cites £55m, the Guardian around £51m (€60m). BBC Sport, in a separate piece on possible replacements, says a move for Rodri now looks increasingly likely. Separately, Chelsea have set Manchester City a 5pm Friday deadline to bid for Enzo Fernandez, per Sky and the Guardian — a deadline, not a completed deal.

At Arsenal, Galatasaray have bid for winger Gabriel Martinelli, reported separately by BBC's gossip column and the Guardian, which puts the offer near £38m; a BBC Sport follow-up on the same story is the same newsroom as the gossip column, not independent confirmation. No fee has been confirmed by the club. Manager Mikel Arteta, per Sky Sports, said Arsenal are still looking to add signings but that only a small pool of available players could improve the squad.

Paris Saint-Germain beat Aston Villa 2-1 to win the UEFA Super Cup, with Desire Doue scoring the winner, per Sky Sports; the same result featured in the Guardian's Football Weekly podcast rundown. That result is settled, not a rumour. Sky separately highlighted a strong debut from 17-year-old Villa forward Brian Madjo in the same match.
