Arsenal's Rout Frames a Shifting Man City, Liverpool

Manchester City open their rebuild on the back foot: Arsenal beat them 3-0 in the Community Shield, a result covered independently by Sky Sports and Opta Analyst. City's midfield is turning over at the same time. The Guardian reports Rodri has flown to Barcelona for a medical and called the move "a dream," describing City's bid as accepted, though no club has confirmed the transfer as done. BBC Gossip and BBC Sport both put Enzo Fernandez's fee above £120m, but as the same newsroom that is one voice, not two.

Arsenal's own market talk stayed speculative but recent: the Guardian reports Atlético may prefer sending Julián Álvarez to Arsenal rather than Barcelona, while BBC Gossip's Monday column said Real Madrid want Arsenal's Martin Zubimendi. Neither is confirmed. Liverpool's ownership moved more concretely: BBC Sport reports a Jeff Bezos-led consortium has bought close to 40% of the club from Fenway Sports Group. Separately, FourFourTwo reports Liverpool have agreed terms with an unnamed alternative to PSG's Bradley Barcola as that deal stalls — a single-source, unconfirmed claim.
