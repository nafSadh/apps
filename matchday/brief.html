Arsenal's Martinelli sale is the day's one settled deal

Arsenal have sold Gabriel Martinelli to Al Hilal for a club-record £60m, per Sky Sports — a completed transfer, not a rumour, and the clearest settled news touching the six clubs tracked here today. By contrast, BBC's gossip column has Arsenal still exploring moves for Julian Alvarez and Lille's Matias Fernandez-Pardo, with Chelsea said to be eyeing Bournemouth's Eli Junior Kroupi. None of that is confirmed, and no second outlet corroborates it independently.

Manchester City's rebuild looks real on the numbers: Opta Analyst puts this summer's outlay on Enzo Fernandez, Anderson and Bouaddi at roughly £300m, and asks whether that replaces Rodri — a question it raises, not answers. BBC Sport separately quotes manager Enzo Maresca saying players wanted updates on the Fernandez deal as the window closed. At Liverpool, Sky Sports reports net spend topped £200m, more than last summer, but still calls the squad imbalanced under Andoni Iraola. Bradley Barcola is set for his league debut against Ipswich on Friday, per Sky Sports and FourFourTwo.

Paris Saint-Germain have drawn both league games to open the season; the Guardian reports Luis Enrique is counting on 'new energy' from the summer signings ahead of Friday's match against Monaco. Real Madrid's only appearance in today's feed is a Transfermarkt data point ranking them sixth for net spend among Europe's 250 clubs this season. There is no Bayern Munich news in today's feed.
