Liverpool rout 10-man Man Utd on Pochettino's debut

Liverpool beat Manchester United 4-0 at Anfield in Mauricio Pochettino's first match in charge of United, a result covered independently by Sky Sports, BBC Sport and the Guardian. United were reduced to ten men after eight minutes when Leny Yoro was sent off for a foul on Liverpool's Alexander Isak. Pochettino and Kobbie Mainoo called the decision unfair; BBC Sport ran a separate piece questioning whether the red card was correct, without resolving it either way.

Manchester City's women's team lost a second straight match, beaten 3-1 by Tottenham, a result reported separately by Sky Sports, the Guardian and BBC Sport; City led through Khadija Shaw before Spurs's late double denied them a return to the top of the WSL table. On the men's side, Arsenal drew 2-2 at Newcastle after what Sky Sports called a complacent second half, while Arsenal's WSL side were booed at home after a sixth successive draw, 0-0 with West Ham, with manager Slegers facing questions about her future, per Sky Sports.

Elsewhere, Sky Sports reported that Paris Saint-Germain have confirmed Doué has an ACL injury and faces imminent surgery. Real Madrid confirmed that Arda Güler, 21, has signed a contract extension through 2032, with Transfermarkt noting his value has grown from a €30m signing fee to a roughly €100m valuation. Transfermarkt separately reported, citing other unnamed outlets, that Real Madrid are expected to trigger a buy-back clause on Nico Paz next summer; that claim is not corroborated elsewhere in this feed.
