Fernández's £125m move to Man City equals transfer record

Enzo Fernández's move from Chelsea to Manchester City is done, not rumoured: the Guardian, Sky Transfers, BBC Sport and Transfermarkt all report it as completed. The Guardian gives a £125m fee that equals the British transfer record; Transfermarkt separately cites €145m. It was the marquee deal of a record-breaking deadline day — Sky Transfers reports Premier League clubs spent £3.4bn this summer, a figure the Guardian says is up 11.9% on last year.

Chelsea's deadline day was otherwise messy. Sky Transfers reported Monaco pulled out of selling midfielder Lamine Camara to Chelsea, unhappy at how a separate deal sending Monaco forward Folarin Balogun to Everton had collapsed; BBC Sport and Transfermarkt both confirmed the Balogun-Everton transfer fell through, with Transfermarkt citing a lost €45m fee. Arsenal's pursuit of Atlético Madrid's Julián Álvarez also ended empty-handed — Sky Transfers reports Álvarez stays at Atlético, leaving Arsenal short of an out-and-out striker.

Liverpool's own window drew a muted verdict: Sky Transfers rated the club's business 'not bad but not great,' without elaborating, while Transfermarkt notes Manchester City's summer outlay now exceeds the single-window spending record Liverpool previously held. Fernández himself told City's in-house media after signing that he expects the club to 'win lots together,' per Sky Sports.
