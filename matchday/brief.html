Arsenal complete Konsa deal as Man City sell Savinho to Spurs

Arsenal have completed a £51m deal to sign Ezri Konsa from Aston Villa, according to Sky Sports. The move was reported independently in the Guardian's live coverage and treated as done in separate analysis pieces from Opta Analyst and FourFourTwo, giving it corroboration across genuinely separate newsrooms. One detail conflicts: FourFourTwo's analysis describes it as a £60m deal, against the £51m figure from Sky and the Guardian. Arsenal, the reigning Premier League champions, host Coventry in Friday's season opener.

Manchester City have agreed an £85m deal to sell Savinho to Tottenham, per Sky Sports, with the same fee cited in a separate Opta Analyst assessment of the move. City manager Enzo Maresca said Savinho had wanted to leave 'from day one.' Separately, the Guardian reports Crystal Palace have told City that midfielder Adam Wharton is not for sale, while Transfermarkt reports Liverpool are targeting the same player — the two outlets point in different directions on his future.

At Bayern Munich, manager Vincent Kompany said a 'slight' change in Jamal Musiala's medication was behind two collapses in four days, according to BBC Sport. It is the only Bayern item in today's feed and carries no outside corroboration.
