Man City guilty verdict overshadows Kane's advancing Bayern deal

An independent commission has found Manchester City guilty of financial rule breaches across nine seasons, Sky Sports reports; the club denies wrongdoing and has launched an appeal. Rival Premier League clubs do not expect City to remain in the division next season if that appeal fails, Sky Sports adds, while pundit Jamie Carragher has called for the case to be resolved quickly either way. The Guardian, Opta Analyst and FourFourTwo each covered the fallout independently, a sign of how significant - and how unresolved - the story still is.

Elsewhere, Bayern Munich's contract talks with Harry Kane are reportedly in their final stages, per Sky Sports, a claim that lines up with the BBC's gossip column, which separately has Kane close to a new deal and reports Bayern have also offered Michael Olise a long-term contract. Real Madrid, meanwhile, are tied to a string of unconfirmed targets - Virgil van Dijk, Jarell Quansah, Alex Scott among them - all via the BBC's gossip column alone, with no other outlet corroborating any of it, so these remain speculation rather than settled business.

On the pitch, Liverpool are unbeaten under new manager Andoni Iraola heading into Sunday's home game against Manchester City, with Sky Sports confirming the referee assignment. Off it, Arsenal's season continues to draw scrutiny: the Guardian and Opta Analyst both flag a difficult start for head coach Renee Slegers, with the Women's Super League side on five points and two goals from two matches, and pressure mounting ahead of their next fixture against Man City.
