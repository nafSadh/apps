Man City go three clear as Real Madrid fall in derby

Manchester City beat Sunderland 5-3 in a chaotic match to move three points clear at the top of the Premier League during the international break, covered independently by Sky Sports, the Guardian, BBC Sport and Opta Analyst. Opta noted Erling Haaland's late goal means he has now scored against all 25 clubs he has faced in the competition. The Guardian said City "sent a message" to Arsenal, whose own unbeaten start ended in a defeat to Brighton that left manager Mikel Arteta admitting his side had been "humbled."

In Madrid, Atlético beat a 10-man Real Madrid 2-1 in a feisty derby after Dean Huijsen was sent off, moving above their rivals into second place in La Liga. Sky Sports, BBC Sport and Opta Analyst each reported the result independently, with Opta crediting Diego Simeone with ending a losing run in the fixture against Real manager José Mourinho. This is a completed result, not a projection.

Liverpool kept their unbeaten start going with a 1-0 win at Bournemouth, Alexander Isak scoring the winner on Andoni Iraola's return to his former club; Sky Sports, the Guardian, BBC Sport and Opta Analyst all covered the match. Separately, BBC's gossip column and Transfermarkt both mention Liverpool watching Netherlands midfielder Kees Smit, but that remains transfer interest, not a reported deal.
