Arsenal end Anfield hoodoo, move three clear at the top

Arsenal beat Liverpool 1-0 at Anfield on Sunday, their first league win there since 2012-13 per Opta Analyst, and sit three points clear atop the Premier League per the Guardian. The match drew genuinely separate coverage from Sky Sports, the Guardian, BBC Sport, Opta Analyst and FourFourTwo, not one newsroom repeated. Notably, every "also" corroboration tag in today's feed links two outlets from the same newsroom, BBC Gossip to BBC Sport, Sky Transfers to Sky Sports, rather than independent confirmation. Kai Havertz scored the winner; Declan Rice was singled out by the Guardian and FourFourTwo.

On the rumour side, Sky Sports reports Harry Kane is "extremely close" to a new Bayern Munich contract, a claim sourced only to Sky despite surfacing twice in its feed. Separately, BBC Sport quotes Kane saying he would swap his Ballon d'Or for a Champions League medal, after Sky's Euro Debrief noted his missed penalty capped a bad week for Bayern. At Manchester City, Sky reports winger Allan has earned a first Brazil call-up, while FourFourTwo, alone, reports City have been offered a route to sell an unnamed "£100m underperformer."
