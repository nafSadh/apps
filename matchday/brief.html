PSG set £145m Barcola price as Vinicius saga continues

Sky Sports and BBC's gossip column both reported this week that Paris Saint-Germain have set a £145m asking price for Bradley Barcola, with Liverpool and Arsenal named as interested parties in both accounts — a valuation, not confirmation of a bid from either club. Separately, Sky reported Real Madrid are confident of signing RB Leipzig's Yan Diomande after PSG withdrew from the race, though that conflicts with BBC's report a day earlier that a Real bid for Diomande had already been rejected.

Vinicius Jr's Real Madrid future is the other thread running across outlets: BBC's gossip column linked Arsenal to a move for the Brazilian on Sunday, and Sky Sports followed with reports that Real will hold further talks with his camp this week while Arsenal are said to be ready to offer him a record contract. All of it remains sourced to reporting rather than any club statement, and no figures or timelines have been given.

Manchester City's Rodri has been the subject of contradictory BBC reports: on July 18 the gossip column said Real Madrid had cooled their interest, then on July 24 the same column said Real were confident of signing him — the two should be read as unresolved rather than a change in Rodri's situation. The one settled item concerning these clubs was Liverpool's: Sky Sports reported Andoni Iraola's first match in charge ended in a 4-2 win over Sunderland in Nashville.
