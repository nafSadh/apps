Barcelona raises Rodri bid, City eyes Fernández move

Barcelona have raised their offer for Manchester City's Rodri to around £51m (€60m), the Guardian reports, calling it close to City's valuation of the Spain midfielder. BBC's gossip column and Sky Sports both tracked the same story through the week, though neither added financial detail beyond the Guardian's figure. The Guardian ties the sale to City's other business: with Rodri gone, City are said to be pursuing Chelsea's Enzo Fernández, whose reported £120m price may be too high to meet.

Liverpool's rebuild stays unsettled. The Guardian reports Bradley Barcola is 'firmly' in their sights to rebuild the attack after Mohamed Salah's departure, echoing BBC gossip reports earlier in the week that a fee for the winger has yet to be agreed. The same BBC column noted Liverpool have also explored Ibrahim Mbaye and ruled out any move for Djed Spence. Separately, Sky Sports says Inter are readying a fresh bid for Liverpool's Jones, with no further detail given, and an Opta Analyst squad audit judged Liverpool 'still look short in certain areas' before the window closes.

PSG face Aston Villa in the UEFA Super Cup in Salzburg, a fixture previewed at length by Sky Sports, FourFourTwo and Opta Analyst, with Khvicha Kvaratskhelia's return from World Cup absence a recurring theme in that coverage. On the Arsenal side, Sky Sports alone reports Chelsea have made a fresh enquiry for Myles Lewis-Skelly — a single-source claim with no word yet on Arsenal's response.
