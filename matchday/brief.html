BBC and Transfermarkt both link Arsenal to Endrick

The best-corroborated line in today's feed is Arsenal's interest in Real Madrid forward Endrick. BBC's Wednesday gossip column and a separate Transfermarkt report both carried the story on November 10 — genuine convergence from two different outlets. BBC's own listed corroboration for that piece is BBC Sport, which is the same newsroom and doesn't count as independent confirmation; Transfermarkt running the same link separately does. Endrick to Arsenal remains speculation, not a confirmed deal, and Real Madrid have not commented.

One Arsenal story is settled rather than rumoured: an independent panel has ruled, per Sky Sports, that Liverpool should have had a penalty when Jurrien Timber pulled Alexander Isak's shirt during Arsenal's win at Anfield — an official finding, not speculation. Arsenal's midfield picture stays unsettled on the rumour side: Sky reports Martin Zubimendi has responded to January exit talk, while BBC separately names Atletico Madrid as suitors and Sky says Chelsea have joined the race for an Arsenal midfielder, each from a single outlet.

Elsewhere, BBC's gossip column links both Real Madrid and PSG to Aston Villa goalkeeper Zion Suzuki, and separately reports Bayern Munich remain in talks over Rio Ngumoha while facing Barcelona's interest in defender Jonathan Tah — all single-source and unconfirmed. For Liverpool, FourFourTwo reports a 'deal in place' for their first January signing, also unconfirmed. Man City's only mention is a single-source FourFourTwo report describing Kevin De Bruyne, since leaving for Napoli, as an early-stage target for Everton.
