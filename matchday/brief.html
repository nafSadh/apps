Rivals line up legal claim against City as Bayern struggle

The clearest story today is off the pitch. Manchester United, Liverpool and Tottenham have retained the same law firm, Wiggin, to prepare a legal claim against Manchester City over alleged financial breaches, the Guardian reports, with damages potentially running into the hundreds of millions of pounds. It is single-sourced, with no second outlet confirming it, but it describes action already under way rather than a rumour, which sets it apart from today's transfer chatter.

On the pitch, Bayern Munich's poor run continued with a 2-1 Champions League defeat to Lille, covered twice by the Guardian, which pointed to mounting injuries under Vincent Kompany. PSG have an injury problem of their own: Transfermarkt reports wingers Khvicha Kvaratskhelia (ACL) and Doue are both out, leaving the club assessing winger options for the January window during a Champions League title defence. Both items are single-sourced.

Everything else is unconfirmed transfer gossip. BBC's gossip column links Arsenal and PSG with RB Leipzig winger Antonio Nusa, says Liverpool are exploring a deal for Bayern's Jamal Musiala, and reports Erling Haaland is considering a Man City exit. That Haaland item ran under both BBC Gossip and BBC Sport bylines, which is the same newsroom repeating itself, not independent confirmation. FourFourTwo's analysis separately suggests City manager Enzo Maresca is under pressure after a poor run of results.
