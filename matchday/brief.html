Arsenal lock in Arteta as City weigh verdict fallout

Mikel Arteta has signed a new long-term contract at Arsenal, confirmed by both Sky Sports and the Guardian, two separate newsrooms, making this the best-attested story of the day. The Guardian puts the deal at more than £80m over four years and says it makes Arteta the Premier League's best-paid manager. Sky Sports covered the signing from several angles, framing it as the next stage of his rebuilding project at the club.

Elsewhere the news is more provisional. BBC Sport reports Declan Rice is close to agreeing a new long-term Arsenal contract, though the deal is not yet done and the story is not matched elsewhere in today's feed. Separately, FourFourTwo's analysis says Manchester City, found guilty of more than 100 financial rule breaches, intend to challenge the Premier League's verdict on at least one point, with one City player quoted saying he hopes not to lose any titles over it; that account is single-sourced.

Transfer talk was heavier on speculation than substance. Transfermarkt reported Kylian Mbappe is unhappy at Real Madrid while PSG are eyeing Rayan Cherki, and separately that Arsenal's Mikel Merino has been linked with a move to Real Madrid. FourFourTwo reported talks between Liverpool and Mbappe over a return to Anfield. None of these carry a second source today; BBC's gossip column separately said Manchester City's Erling Haaland is seen as more likely to join Barcelona than Arsenal if he leaves City.
