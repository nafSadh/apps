Pochettino's bow at Anfield is the story that travels

Mauricio Pochettino takes charge of Manchester United for the first time this Sunday, away at Liverpool. The Guardian reports he wants his side to show "no fear" and go to Anfield to win, while Sky Sports separately reported him vowing to "change things" at Old Trafford in his first interview as boss. BBC Sport and Sky Sports both reported, independently, that footage of his first training session had been filmed by a drone and leaked online.

Manchester City's off-field troubles were the best-corroborated story involving the big six: Sky Sports and the Guardian both reported Premier League clubs voted to extend legal indemnity to board members, cover explicitly tied to the risk of City's sponsors suing over the club's rules case. FourFourTwo separately claimed City face a further financial hit from the Premier League charges, but that report stands alone and is unconfirmed elsewhere in today's feed.

Arsenal's Women's team fell further behind Lyon in the Champions League, with Sky Sports and the Guardian both running live coverage of the same match. On the men's side, BBC's gossip column reported Arsenal may need to pay RB Leipzig £66m for Antonio Nusa - an attributed rumour, not confirmed. Real Madrid's news was more settled: per Transfermarkt, the club has confirmed Arda Guler signed a contract extension to 2032, with his valuation now put at around 100m euros, up from a 30m-euro signing fee.
