City beat 10-man Liverpool at Anfield, go top

Manchester City beat a 10-man Liverpool 3-0 at Anfield, their first match since an independent commission found the club guilty of all charges over breaches of the Premier League's financial rules, a verdict City have said they are appealing. The result sends City top of the table. The Guardian, BBC Sport, Sky Sports and Opta Analyst all filed match reports on the win.

Elsewhere among the six, results were mixed and rumours stayed rumours. Real Madrid scraped past Villarreal 1-0 but had Vinicius Junior sent off for hair-pulling, per the Guardian. Arsenal came from behind to beat Leeds 2-1, per Opta Analyst. On transfers, BBC Gossip and Transfermarkt separately link Real Madrid with a move for Scott McTominay, but neither calls it done. Bayern's Harry Kane situation is similarly unsettled: BBC Gossip had him closing in on a new deal, and Transfermarkt's live blog says Bayern have since issued a statement on his contract, without detail.
