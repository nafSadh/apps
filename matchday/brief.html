Arsenal's Zubimendi linked with Chelsea and Real Madrid

BBC's gossip column reports that Real Madrid and Chelsea are keen on Arsenal midfielder Martin Zubimendi, who may look to leave in January. The story also ran on BBC Sport, but that is the same newsroom rather than a second outlet, so it is best read as a single unconfirmed report rather than a corroborated one. BBC's gossip column separately said Liverpool's Federico Chiesa could leave the club, with his future described as in doubt — again a single-sourced rumour, not settled business.

On confirmed business, Chelsea have handed winger Pedro Neto a new contract after he turned down a move to Manchester City in the summer, the Guardian reports. Transfermarkt separately looked back at why Bayern Munich walked away from moves for Anthony Gordon and Bradley Barcola, a combined €205m in deals that never went through — both pieces cover already-settled outcomes rather than live speculation.

Ahead of this weekend's Manchester derby, Sky's Paul Merson called it a defining game for City and United, and City manager Enzo Maresca backed Elliot Anderson's 'kings of Manchester' remark, with the Guardian noting Nico O'Reilly is due to return. Sky separately argued City's midfield depth has softened the loss of Rodri, who left the club over the summer. Arsenal's Mikel Arteta said Jurriën Timber and Cristhian Mosquera could return against Sunderland, and Sky's live blog reports Liverpool's Bradley Barcola should be available for the Fulham game.
