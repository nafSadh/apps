Rodri Nears Barcelona As City Rebuild After Rout

Rodri's move to Barcelona is the best-corroborated story in today's feed: the BBC, Sky Sports, the Guardian and Transfermarkt have all reported Manchester City accepting a bid for the midfielder, and Sky adds he is now set for a medical. The reported fee is £65.4m, though Transfermarkt separately cites €76.5m — neither club has confirmed the transfer as complete.

The exit comes as City look to rebuild after Sunday's 3-0 Community Shield defeat to Arsenal, a result reported independently by Opta Analyst, Sky Sports, the Guardian and Transfermarkt. The BBC and Sky both report City are in advanced talks for Lille's Ayyoub Bouaddi as a Rodri replacement, and FourFourTwo and Sky both say City remain interested in Chelsea's Enzo Fernandez despite missing a bid deadline.

BBC's gossip column has linked Arsenal midfielder Martin Zubimendi with a departure this week — to Chelsea earlier on, to Real Madrid by Monday — but both versions trace to the same single source, with no other outlet corroborating either destination. Treat it as unconfirmed speculation, not an active move.
