Arsenal and Liverpool win Champions League openers

Arsenal opened their Champions League campaign with a 1-0 win at Napoli, settled by a Martin Ødegaard strike that manager Mikel Arteta attributed to the captain's improved fitness and a new role, according to Sky Transfers and Opta Analyst. Liverpool also won, coming from behind to beat Atlético Madrid 2-1 on an Alexis Mac Allister goal, though winger Bradley Barcola limped off injured, per FourFourTwo. Elsewhere in the competition, Barcelona thrashed Feyenoord 5-1 behind a Raphinha brace, and PSG beat Slovan Bratislava with a Torres hat-trick, per the Guardian.

Off the pitch, BBC Sport reports Arsenal are close to finalizing a new contract for Arteta, though that is attributed to a single source and not yet confirmed as done. Separately, Transfermarkt notes Bayern Munich walked away from moves for Anthony Gordon and Bradley Barcola; both ultimately signed elsewhere, with Barcola joining Liverpool and Gordon moving to Barcelona, where sporting director Deco said this week the winger's style suited Hansi Flick's side better than Marcus Rashford's would have.

Rumour columns floated interest in Real Madrid's Endrick from Italy, and BBC's gossip column separately said Liverpool and Manchester United are both watching Germany international Malick Thiaw — none of it confirmed. Most items marked as corroborated in today's feed are not independent: BBC Gossip's stories are echoed only by BBC Sport, and Sky's transfer gossip only by Sky Sports — same newsrooms, not separate confirmation. There was no notable movement today involving Manchester City beyond a Rodri apology for calling Valencia 'weak,' per Sky Transfers.
