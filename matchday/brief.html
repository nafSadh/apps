City's perfect start ends; Arsenal level, Liverpool up to 4th

Manchester City's unbeaten start under Enzo Maresca ended Saturday with a 2-1 loss at Aston Villa, according to Opta Analyst's match stats. Arsenal capitalized, beating Everton 2-0 to move level with City on points. Afterward, manager Mikel Arteta told Sky Sports he is now compiling footage of rival Premier League managers leaving their technical areas, after picking up his own second yellow card of the season for doing the same during the Everton match.

Liverpool's 2-0 win over Brighton was the best-corroborated story of the weekend among the watched clubs: BBC, Sky Sports, the Guardian and Opta Analyst each filed separate match coverage, not just a shared wire piece. Alexander Isak scored for a third consecutive Premier League game, and the win moved Liverpool up to fourth in the table, ending Brighton's run of good form, per the Guardian.

Real Madrid's next fixture, Sunday's Clasico at Barcelona, drew separate previews from BBC Sport, Opta Analyst and Transfermarkt -- Jose Mourinho's first Clasico against Barcelona in 13 years, per Opta. None of those pieces report a result, so the match remains unsettled in this feed. On the transfer side, a single BBC gossip roundup from earlier in the week listed Real Madrid as still keeping an eye on Arsenal's Riccardo Calafiori -- an unconfirmed line from one source, not corroborated elsewhere.
