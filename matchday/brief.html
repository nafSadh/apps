Kompany illness clouds Bayern's clash with Arsenal

The clearest story in today's feed is doubt over Vincent Kompany's availability for Wednesday's Champions League visit of Arsenal to the Allianz Arena. Sky, the Guardian and FourFourTwo each reported, independently, that the Bayern Munich manager is battling a virus affecting his legs. The Guardian adds that his assistant is poised to take charge on the touchline if Kompany cannot make it; none of the three has said he is confirmed out.

Tuesday's Champions League card brought mostly team news rather than drama, per live coverage from Sky and the Guardian: Liverpool left Alexander Isak, Florian Wirtz and Bradley Barcola on the bench against Villarreal at Anfield, and Manchester City started 17-year-old academy product Samba against AEK Athens. Opta Analyst notes Isak has scored the winner in three straight Premier League away games, including Saturday's 2-1 win at Brentford. The BBC previewed Paris St-Germain's trip to Barcelona as a meeting of the competition's current top two.

Everything involving Real Madrid stays in rumour territory. Transfermarkt's transfer-news blog floated a report that Trent Alexander-Arnold could leave the club, with Barcelona linked to Real's young defender Manzambi -- a single-outlet claim, unconfirmed elsewhere in the feed. Separately, Sky's Paul Merson column argues Arsenal's defeat to Brighton has 'scared' the team and predicts they will not beat Bayern on Wednesday -- a pundit's opinion, not a report.
