Liverpool's Hughes departure is the day's firmest story

Liverpool's sporting director Richard Hughes is leaving the club, with Sky, the Guardian and BBC Sport all reporting it independently — three separate newsrooms rather than one story reprinted. The Guardian says Liverpool have confirmed his exit and that FSG president Mike Gordon is leading the search for a replacement, the club's fourth sporting director in four years. Hughes is expected to take a similar role at Saudi club Al-Hilal, per the Guardian.

Results moved more than transfers did elsewhere among the six. Real Madrid's perfect La Liga start ended in a 1-0 loss at Real Betis, substitute Troy Parrott scoring the winner; the same Guardian report says Paris Saint-Germain were beaten by league leaders Monaco, with a late Kylian Mbappe penalty saved by Valles. Neither result is corroborated elsewhere in today's feed. Manchester City beat Coventry 1-0 on an Erling Haaland goal, with Enzo Fernandez — signed for a British record-equalling £125m — impressing on his full debut, per the Guardian and Sky.

On Arsenal, BBC's gossip column has the club still exploring a late move for Julian Alvarez and considering Barcelona's Raphinha, alongside interest in Bournemouth's Eli Junior Kroupi.
