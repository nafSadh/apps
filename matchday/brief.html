Arsenal's Carabao Cup Shock Leads a Quiet Day

Arsenal's Carabao Cup exit to League Two side Fleetwood Town was the most widely covered story in today's feed, reported independently by Sky Sports, the Guardian, FourFourTwo and Opta Analyst. The result itself is settled. Still playing out is the fallout from Max Dowman's red card: Arsenal is expected to appeal, though Sky's Dermot Gallagher argued on Ref Watch that the dismissal will stand, and Arteta has since discussed his tunnel reaction directly with the 16-year-old, per Sky and FourFourTwo.

The Guardian covered the same episode separately, framing it as evidence of how sensitive Mao-era history remains in Chinese state media. The two outlets are reporting independently on the same story, making this the best-sourced item involving any of the six clubs tracked here outside of Arsenal's cup exit.

Everything else is thin and unconfirmed.
