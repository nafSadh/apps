Wirtz rumours persist; Man City presses VAR complaint

Florian Wirtz's future at Liverpool was a recurring theme this week: BBC's gossip column reported Bayern Munich are monitoring the midfielder, and by midweek Transfermarkt and FourFourTwo were separately writing that Bayern were considering an offer. None of the three outlets cite a fee, timeline, or contact between the clubs, and the reports remain speculative rather than confirmed interest from either side.

Manchester City manager Enzo Maresca said the club are still waiting for an apology over the VAR review of Erling Haaland's winner in the Manchester derby; BBC Sport quoted him calling it "quite poor" that the row has overshadowed City's win at Manchester United. The Guardian separately reported the same waiting-for-apology comments, making this the best-corroborated City story of the day, though it concerns an officiating dispute rather than squad news.

Real Madrid's players covered up a shirt bearing a message of support for Ceuta ahead of their match with Elche, per BBC Sport; the Guardian's Football Daily newsletter separately described an "awkward night" involving T-shirt trouble and an Elche scare. Separately, Arsenal's Mikel Merino compared teenage forward Max Dowman to Lamine Yamal after another scoring display, a story run by both Sky Sports and the Guardian.
