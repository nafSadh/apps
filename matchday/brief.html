PSG complete Torres signing as Liverpool sale is sealed

Paris Saint-Germain have completed the signing of Ferran Torres from Barcelona, confirmed by Sky Sports, BBC Sport, the Guardian and Transfermarkt — the most corroborated story in today's feed. Sky and Transfermarkt put the fee near £42.7m; the Guardian instead cites a €50m fee on a contract running to 2031. Torres, who scored the winning goal in Spain's World Cup final, is described by BBC Sport and the Guardian alike as PSG's marquee arrival of the window.

Liverpool's ownership change is also done, not rumoured. Fenway Sports Group has sold roughly 30% of the club to a consortium including Amazon founder Jeff Bezos, per Sky Sports and the Guardian, which values the club at about £5.5bn and installs consortium figure Amit Bhatia as vice-chairman. Both outlets describe the sale as sealed, with no terms disputed between the two accounts.

Arsenal's business stays speculative. BBC's gossip column reports the club leading the race to sign Jack Hinshelwood and says Barcelona are preparing a third bid for Rodri, whom Manchester City have already rejected bids for this week — claims echoed only by BBC Sport, the same newsroom, so not independent confirmation. Separately, Mikel Arteta told the Guardian his own contract situation "is not going to be an issue," a single-sourced comment with no extension reported as agreed.
