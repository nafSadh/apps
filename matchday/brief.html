Arsenal end 14-year Anfield wait as Real Madrid roll on

Arsenal won at Anfield for the first time in 14 years on Sunday, beating Liverpool in what Opta Analyst listed as the Premier League's only fixture of the round, Sky Sports reported. The Guardian and FourFourTwo also covered the match, though in today's feed only Sky's report states the result. It is the clearest news of the day for the six clubs tracked here: a direct Liverpool-Arsenal result rather than a rumour.

Elsewhere, Real Madrid won 3-0 at Racing Santander for a sixth straight win, with Kylian Mbappé scoring a penalty and Bellingham and Diomandé also scoring, the Guardian reported. Bayern Munich drew 2-2 with Borussia Dortmund in the Klassiker; Harry Kane missed a penalty and a Silva goal secured the point, per Opta Analyst. Manchester City lost at home to Brighton for the first time ever, a second straight defeat for the Premier League's leaders, also per Opta Analyst.

On the transfer side, Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta said a Declan Rice deal was 'only a matter of time,' Sky Sports reported, with no further detail on its nature. BBC's gossip column separately said three unnamed Premier League clubs are watching Bayern teenager Lennart Karl — attributed speculation, not a confirmed move. In the Women's Super League, Arsenal's 1-1 draw with Aston Villa added to pressure on manager Slegers, per the Guardian, while Manchester City's women beat London City Lionesses.
