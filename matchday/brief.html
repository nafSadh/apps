Man City confirms appeal as case fallout spreads

Manchester City's guilty verdict from the Premier League's independent commission stands; the club's appeal against it is now confirmed, separately, by BBC Sport and Sky Sports. The Guardian and Sky Sports, also reporting independently, say a 2015 statement from sponsor Etihad Airways to the US government appears to contradict City's defence, raising questions about the appeal strategy itself.

Fallout continues around the case. Per Sky Sports, Premier League players and agents are exploring possible compensation claims over lost bonus payments, and the Treasury Committee has written to HMRC about the tax implications of City's breaches. England manager Thomas Tuchel, in comments covered separately by BBC Sport and the Guardian, said City's England players are worried about their futures, and that he would not rule out picking players from outside the top flight if City are relegated.

Among the tracked clubs, Arsenal's Christos Tzolis faces a spell out after a hamstring injury on international duty, reported by Sky Sports and, separately, by FourFourTwo; William Saliba also remains out, per FourFourTwo. Real Madrid are reported by the BBC's gossip column, and separately by Transfermarkt, to be monitoring Bayer Leverkusen's Jarell Quansah — a rumour, not confirmed by either club.
