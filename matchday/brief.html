Man City's guilty verdict dominates; Bayern nears Kane deal

An independent commission has found Manchester City guilty on all charges related to financial rule-breaking across nine seasons, Sky and BBC Sport both report. City are appealing; rival Premier League clubs want retrospective and future punishment, according to BBC, and UEFA says it is monitoring the case before deciding on any action of its own, according to Sky. No sanction has been finalized while the appeal is pending.

Bayern Munich's Harry Kane is close to a contract extension, with talks in their final stages per Sky, and separately reported as nearing a new deal by BBC's gossip column. That BBC item also ran as BBC Sport content — the same newsroom, not independent confirmation — and neither report is an official announcement. The gossip column also links Real Madrid to a free Virgil van Dijk and Bayer Leverkusen's Jarell Quansah, and reports Spanish and Italian suitors for Arsenal's Noni Madueke; none of it is confirmed by any club.

Elsewhere, Transfermarkt data shows Vinicius Junior's market value down sharply over the past 18 months, with an Arsenal move still floated as a destination; Real Madrid's squad otherwise remains the most valuable in La Liga, per the same source. Liverpool host Manchester City at Anfield on Sunday in the week's marquee Premier League fixture, per Sky, which also flags a fitness question over Liverpool's Jacquet ahead of kickoff.
