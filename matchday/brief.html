Arsenal fall to Brighton as Kane breaks Bundesliga record

Arsenal's unbeaten Premier League start ended on Saturday, beaten 3-0 at Brighton for their first league defeat of the season. The BBC, the Guardian and Sky Sports each ran independent match reports, and Opta's stats page attributes the result to Brighton's pressing. Manager Mikel Arteta told the BBC his side were 'very far' from the standard required to compete. This is a settled result, confirmed across multiple independent outlets, not a transfer rumour.

This is a completed result rather than a projection, though it is reported by a single outlet in today's feed.
