Arsenal's penalty row and Foden's red mark the weekend

Arsenal beat Sunderland 2-0 to keep their unbeaten start intact, with goals from Bruno Guimarães and Bukayo Saka and a penalty save from David Raya, according to Opta Analyst's match stats. The win was overshadowed by the penalty Sunderland were awarded: Sky Sports and the Guardian both reported Mikel Arteta calling the decision "unacceptable," arguing it should never have stood. The penalty call itself remains disputed, not settled fact, but Arteta's reaction is corroborated by two independent outlets.

In the Manchester derby, City took a lead through Erling Haaland after a VAR review, and United's Phil Foden was sent off following a clash with Bruno Fernandes. Sky Sports, BBC Sport and the Guardian each filed independent coverage of the match. Pundits split on the red card: Sky's Roy Keane argued Foden should not have gone and criticized Fernandes' role in it, while other coverage treated the decision as contested rather than clear-cut. The feed does not report a final score.

Liverpool's stutter continued with a goalless draw at home to Fulham, their third league draw in four games under new manager Andoni Iraola, per Opta Analyst. Separately, BBC's gossip column — its only source — said Federico Chiesa's Liverpool future is in doubt, that Liverpool are watching defender Malick Thiaw, that Real Madrid's Endrick is drawing interest from Italy, and that Chelsea and Real Madrid both want Arsenal's Martin Zubimendi. None of that is confirmed elsewhere. Real Madrid's settled news: the Guardian reported Kylian Mbappé scored twice as Madrid cruised over the weekend.
