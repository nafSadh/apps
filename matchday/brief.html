Savio completes Spurs move; Man City chase Gakpo

Tottenham have officially completed the signing of Savinho from Manchester City for an initial £75m, rising to around £85m with add-ons, confirmed by the Guardian, BBC Sport and Sky Sports. The winger has reverted to his registered name, Savio, on arrival. Opta Analyst's data review calls the fee a gamble worth taking given his output at City. The move is one part of a busier day of City business rather than an isolated deal.

Manchester City have also agreed a £34m deal for Palmeiras winger Allan Elias, per the Guardian, while Newcastle's move for outgoing City forward Nico Gonzalez was described as 'nearing' by the Guardian on Monday and reported as 'agreed' in BBC's Tuesday gossip column; Transfermarkt notes City stand to recoup most of the fee they paid for him 18 months ago. Sky, BBC and the Guardian all separately report City interest in Liverpool's Cody Gakpo, with Tottenham also credited as suitors and Sky reporting Liverpool do not want to sell.

Arsenal and Al Hilal have made progress on a deal for Gabriel Martinelli, per Sky Sports; that report stands alone in the feed, and FourFourTwo argues Arsenal should keep him regardless. Liverpool are reported by FourFourTwo to be pressing for a deal for Yankuba Minteh, a pursuit also flagged in Monday's BBC gossip column, while Jamie Carragher continues to question, per Sky, where new signing Florian Wirtz fits into Andoni Iraola's side.
