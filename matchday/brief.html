Arsenal draw at Bayern, but lose Saka to injury

Arsenal's 2-2 draw at Bayern Munich was the most widely covered story among these six clubs today. Declan Rice's late equaliser denied Harry Kane a winner, per Opta Analyst's match stats. The bigger concern is Bukayo Saka, substituted early with a quad injury; manager Mikel Arteta said it "doesn't look good at all" (Sky Sports), and Sky, the Guardian and FourFourTwo each covered the fallout separately, with Sky naming Noni Madueke and Max Dowman as options to cover for him.

Paris Saint-Germain, the Champions League holders, beat Barcelona 4-2 on Tuesday but will be without Khvicha Kvaratskhelia "for some time" through injury, per the Guardian's matchday review, which also noted sparse attendance at Manchester City's Champions League fixture and a breakout night for Liverpool teenager Rio Ngumoha at Anfield. Separately, the Guardian reported Man City manager Enzo Maresca saying frank conversations with Phil Foden have revived his form — four goals in his last two games — ahead of Saturday's trip to Aston Villa.

Real Madrid's attacking picture is shifting too: with Vinicius Jr suspended, the BBC reports Jose Mourinho now has more options in the final third, including Endrick, Yan Diomande and Carlos Espi. Less certain is a single FourFourTwo report suggesting Trent Alexander-Arnold could make a surprise return to the Premier League, with unnamed Liverpool rivals interested, framed as Alexander-Arnold having failed to win over Mourinho at Madrid. That claim comes from one outlet only and should be read as speculative rather than confirmed.
