City's unbeaten run ends as Arsenal draw level at the top

Manchester City's perfect start to the season is over after a 2-1 loss at Aston Villa, Nicolas Jackson scoring twice; the Guardian, BBC Sport and Opta Analyst all covered the match independently, with BBC and Opta flagging defensive errors from Donnarumma and Gueli. Arsenal capitalised, beating Everton 2-0 through Konsa and Gabriel to move level with City on points, a result reported across the same three outlets.

Bayern Munich said head coach Vincent Kompany, hospitalised after collapsing in training on Friday, will need "weeks, not days" to recover from a virus, with assistant Aaron Danks taking charge against Freiburg. The Guardian and BBC Sport reported this independently, both citing director Max Eberl. Separately, the Guardian reports Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta is concerned about Bukayo Saka's durability and that the winger, hurt in the win over Bayern, could face up to six weeks out -- a figure not corroborated elsewhere in today's feed.
