Saka out until Christmas; Real Madrid lock up Güler

Arsenal's Bukayo Saka may not return before Christmas, manager Mikel Arteta said on Saturday, with Martin Zubimendi also missing for the trip to Newcastle. The Guardian and Sky Sports both carried the news citing Arteta directly, making it one of the firmer items in a day otherwise dominated by speculation. Separately, BBC's gossip column reported Arsenal are targeting RB Leipzig's Antonio Nusa for a fee that could reach £66m, and FourFourTwo reported a further Arsenal signing is being lined up that would require squad sales — neither of those is confirmed.

Real Madrid confirmed a new long-term contract for Arda Güler through 2032, Transfermarkt reported — a completed move, not a rumour. Reports conflict, though, on Nico Paz: BBC's gossip column has described Manchester United as pursuing the Como midfielder, while Transfermarkt separately reported Real Madrid are preparing to trigger an €80m buy-back clause on him next summer. The two accounts point in different directions, and neither corroborates the other.

Manchester City have lost three Premier League games in a row, the Guardian noted ahead of the weekend fixtures, and Sky Sports reported manager Enzo Maresca is also facing questions over Enzo Fernandez's role in midfield. FourFourTwo separately reported City were found to have broken more than 100 Premier League rules and face a £1.5bn financial penalty, though no other outlet in the feed has corroborated that report. At Liverpool, Virgil van Dijk told Sky Sports he does not yet know if he will stay at Anfield beyond this season, and head coach Andoni Iraola said training-ground drone filming should be illegal.
