Man City and Real Madrid win Champions League openers

Manchester City opened their Champions League campaign with a 2-0 win at Porto, Erling Haaland scoring both goals, while Real Madrid beat Inter 2-1 at the Bernabéu behind goals from Mbappé and Valverde, according to Opta Analyst's match reports. Arsenal traveled to Napoli and Liverpool hosted Atlético Madrid for their own openers, covered live by Sky Sports, but the feed carries no final score for either match.

Liverpool's Alexis Mac Allister said he is "very sad" not to have been offered a new contract and admitted he had "options to leave" over the summer, Sky Sports reported; BBC Sport says the comments have split opinion among Liverpool fans. Sky's live blog also reported a new £300m main shirt sponsor starting next season, though that item, like the contract story, comes from a single outlet's rolling coverage rather than independent confirmation.

Transfermarkt's live blog has Liverpool being offered Dani Carvajal and Arsenal weighing a move for Ivan Toney, and separately reports Manchester United eyeing Malick Thiaw while asking whether Viktor Gyökeres will leave Arsenal; BBC's gossip column also names Thiaw as a target being watched by both Liverpool and Manchester United. None of it is confirmed, and nothing in today's feed was corroborated by an outlet outside the same newsroom. Separately, the Guardian's Ballon d'Or power rankings put Bayern Munich's Harry Kane top of the shortlist on 61 goals in 51 games this season.
