Arsenal draw Real Madrid and Bayern in Champions League

Thursday's Champions League league-phase draw was the best-corroborated story of the day, reported independently by the BBC, Sky, the Guardian, Opta Analyst and FourFourTwo. Arsenal were drawn against Real Madrid and Bayern Munich; Manchester City drew Paris Saint-Germain and Barcelona, with Aston Villa also getting PSG; Liverpool travel to Inter Milan, per the Guardian. This is the confirmed fixture list, not a rumor.

On transfers, Sky Sports reported that Liverpool have agreed a deal in principle worth £120m for PSG forward Bradley Barcola, a fee also carried by Transfermarkt's live transfer feed — genuine corroboration from two separate outlets. That is an agreement between clubs, not an announced, completed transfer; Sky's own Liverpool live-blog was still polling fans on the move hours later. No comparable transfer movement was reported today for Bayern, Real Madrid or Arsenal.

For Manchester City, reported interest in Chelsea's Enzo Fernández continued — the BBC's gossip column and Sky's Friday papers round-up both repeated the story — but it remains speculation, not a confirmed move. Separately, the Guardian and Transfermarkt both report that Omar Marmoush's season-long loan from City to Tottenham is complete, following City's earlier £75m departure of Sávio to Spurs. Chelsea, meanwhile, left Fernández out of Thursday's Carabao Cup win over Luton, per the Guardian.
