Mac Allister's contract angst, Liverpool's new sponsor

Alexis Mac Allister said he is "very sad" that Liverpool have not offered him a new contract, and that he had "options to leave" the club in the summer — remarks reported independently by both Sky Sports and the Guardian, rather than a single-outlet story. Separately, Sky Sports and FourFourTwo both reported Liverpool are lining up a new front-of-shirt sponsor worth around £300m a season, which Sky called the biggest such deal in the club's history.

The 2026 Ballon d'Or nominees were confirmed, with Real Madrid's Jude Bellingham and Bayern Munich's Harry Kane on the men's shortlist alongside Declan Rice — carried by the BBC, the Guardian, Transfermarkt and Opta Analyst. This is the confirmed nominee list, not speculation; the Guardian reports the ceremony moves from Paris to London this year, with a winner announced on 26 October, per Opta Analyst.

Elsewhere, Opta Analyst highlighted Arsenal's Martin Ødegaard for making more attacking runs into the box this season, and Sky Sports profiled Riccardo Calafiori's form ahead of Arsenal's Champions League trip to Napoli — both single-outlet features, not corroborated elsewhere. The Guardian also reported that Cavan Sullivan, a Philadelphia Union teenager on his way to Man City, is getting his longest run of first-team minutes since turning professional.
