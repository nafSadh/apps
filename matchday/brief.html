Man City lose WSL top spot; rumours swirl elsewhere

Manchester City's clearest news of the day was a result, not a rumour: a 1-0 loss at Chelsea in the Women's Super League, with Sandy Baltimore's first-half strike settling it and City reduced to ten players in the second half. Chelsea move back above City at the top of the table. The Guardian, BBC Sport and Sky Sports all reported the same result independently — three separate newsrooms rather than one story being passed around.

Elsewhere for City, FourFourTwo reports a hearing date has been set for the club's appeal against its Premier League sanctions case, though that is a single-source account. BBC's gossip column separately has City and Manchester United competing for Chelsea's Malo Gusto, and has Arsenal linked with Bournemouth's Alex Scott, Atletico Madrid's Martin Zubimendi and Real Madrid forward Endrick. None of these transfer items are corroborated beyond that one column.

Real Madrid are reported by Transfermarkt to be reviving interest in Michael Olise, again from a single outlet. Bayern Munich are said, per the same BBC column, to remain in talks over Rio Ngumoha and to be fending off Barcelona's interest in defender Jonathan Tah. Liverpool are reported to be considering Brighton goalkeeper Bart Verbruggen, with right-back Jeremie Frimpong's exit described as not imminent. All of the above is unconfirmed rumour, not reported fact.
