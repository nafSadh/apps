Maresca signals Man City will miss Fernández deadline

Chelsea gave Manchester City a 5pm Friday deadline to bid for midfielder Enzo Fernández. City manager Enzo Maresca said, "I don't think we're going to do something today," according to the Guardian, and Sky Sports and BBC Sport separately reported no bid had landed as the deadline approached — BBC called it a "pivotal day" for both clubs. Nobody reports the move as dead, only stalled, with City yet to act.

Arsenal are separately in talks with Bayer Leverkusen over defender Jarell Quansah, described by BBC Sport as a former Liverpool player, in reporting that also comes independently from Sky Sports and the Guardian's live blog — three separate newsrooms on the same discussions. None of the three describes an agreed fee or a completed transfer; each frames it as talks only, nothing signed.

On confirmed business, Liverpool completed the permanent departure of midfielder Sofie Lundgaard to Eintracht Frankfurt for an undisclosed fee, per BBC Sport. Separately, Manchester City rejected a renewed Barcelona bid for Rodri, but that comes only from BBC's gossip column and is not corroborated elsewhere in today's feed — treat it as a rumour, not a confirmed rejection.
