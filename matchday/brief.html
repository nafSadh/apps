United to appeal Yoro red card as fallout continues

The clearest story to move today is the fallout from Manchester United's derby defeat under new manager Mauricio Pochettino, with BBC Sport, the Guardian and FourFourTwo - three independent newsrooms - all examining how far Liverpool and United have drifted apart. United have told the FA they intend to appeal Leny Yoro's red card from that match, per Sky Sports; the appeal's outcome is not yet known.

Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta said David Raya's shoulder injury, sustained against Newcastle, is 'not as bad as first feared' and will not need surgery - reported independently by Sky Sports and the Guardian. Raya is set to miss the Dortmund game but could return for the Man City fixture. At Man City, Kevin de Bruyne spoke publicly for the first time since the club's Premier League financial-rules guilty verdict, saying his feelings for the club 'will never change', per BBC Sport and Sky Sports.

Bayern Munich's week has been turbulent, per the Guardian, which examined fallout from a Jamal Musiala dispute; separately, Harry Kane is out for several months with an ankle injury from the draw with Koln, prompting Transfermarkt to speculate on January alternatives - not a confirmed move. PSG's attack remains stretched by Desire Doue's and Khvicha Kvaratskhelia's ACL injuries, both already on the treatment table, per the Guardian.
