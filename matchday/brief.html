Man City appeal dominates; transfer gossip stays rumour

Manchester City's Premier League financial-rules case produced the week's one settled story among the six clubs. Sky reports City have lodged a formal appeal against an independent commission's finding that they breached financial rules across every charge. The Guardian, reporting separately, says whistleblower Rui Pinto is willing to help UK authorities in exchange for protection, and that further documents on City's affairs could still emerge. Sky also reports a 2015 statement from sponsor Etihad Airways has raised questions over City's appeal strategy.

Everything else involving the six clubs is rumour, not confirmed fact. BBC Gossip reports Erling Haaland is seen as more likely to join Barcelona than Arsenal if he leaves City — also carried by BBC Sport, but that is the same newsroom, not independent confirmation. Separate BBC gossip columns have Real Madrid waiting on Virgil van Dijk as a free agent and keen on Jarell Quansah (also flagged by Transfermarkt), Bayern offering Michael Olise a new contract and eyeing Jules Kounde, Liverpool linked with Kevin Schade, and Arsenal watching Porto's Alberto Costa.

One additional settled item concerns Arsenal: the Guardian reports the club has shelved plans for a major expansion of the Emirates Stadium, which would have raised capacity to 60,704, opting instead for a programme of smaller improvements to hospitality and retail areas. The story runs in the Guardian only, with no second outlet confirming it in today's feed, so it stands on that single source for now.
