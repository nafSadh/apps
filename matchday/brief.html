City's win streak hits seven as FFP appeal looms

Manchester City extended their winning run to seven straight league games, beating Ipswich Town 3-2 with two goals from Erling Haaland, continuing what the Guardian and Sky Sports both describe as a record start for manager Enzo Maresca. Separately, BBC Sport reports City face 'substantial hurdles' in overturning their guilty finding on Premier League financial rules, according to a lawyer it consulted — a single-source legal read on an appeal whose outcome remains unresolved.

Liverpool came from behind to beat a nine-man Brentford 2-1, with Alexander Isak scoring on his return from injury; Sky Sports, the Guardian and Opta Analyst all reported the match, including two Brentford red cards. At Arsenal, the women's team were booed off after a 1-1 draw with Birmingham in the WSL, extending a winless run and deepening pressure on manager Renée Slegers, a story carried independently by both Sky Sports and the Guardian.

On the transfer side, BBC's gossip column reports Real Madrid's Jude Bellingham is ready to commit his future to the club — a claim repeated on BBC Sport, the same newsroom, so not independent confirmation. The same column says Arsenal and Liverpool have been told the asking price for Brentford's Kevin Schade, and that Harry Kane is close to a new Bayern Munich contract; none of this is corroborated elsewhere in today's feed, and no Paris Saint-Germain news appeared.
