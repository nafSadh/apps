Arsenal agree Guimaraes fee; Salah set to leave Liverpool

Arsenal's move for Bruno Guimaraes is the best-attested story in today's feed. BBC's gossip column reported a fee agreed with Newcastle on July 31, and Sky Sports independently reported the number this week: a £75m deal for the Newcastle captain. Both are attributed transfer reports rather than club confirmation. Sky separately notes the sale is part of a wider Newcastle clearout worth more than £240m this summer.

Liverpool's clearest news is a departure. Sky Sports reports Mohamed Salah has mutually agreed to terminate his contract a year early and is set to join Trabzonspor on a free transfer, corroborating an earlier BBC report that he was closing in on the same move. Separately, and so far only on Sky's word, Liverpool have explored terms for Paris Saint-Germain's teenage winger Ibrahim Mbaye, which Sky says is independent of the long-stalled, unresolved pursuit of PSG's Bradley Barcola.

Real Madrid's picture is murkier. BBC's gossip column and a separate Guardian analysis piece both describe Madrid as circling Manchester City's Rodri, with the Guardian arguing new City boss Enzo Maresca must prioritize keeping him - but no fee or bid has been reported, only sustained interest. On Yan Diomande, reports conflict: BBC said Tuesday that RB Leipzig were holding out, while Sky described Madrid as finalising a deal the next day.
