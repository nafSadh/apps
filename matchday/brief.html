City's Bouaddi deal leads a Sunday of rumours

Manchester City have agreed a deal with Lille for teenage midfielder Ayyoub Bouaddi, independently reported by the BBC, Sky Sports and the Guardian. BBC and Sky put the total at roughly £86m (€100m); the Guardian breaks that down as €95m (£81.4m) guaranteed plus €5m in add-ons. All three describe it as an agreement in principle rather than an official announcement, and Transfermarkt separately reports the fee would make Bouaddi the most expensive U19 player in history.

At Real Madrid, José Mourinho marked his return as head coach with a 2-1 win at Espanyol, per the Guardian, and separately told the BBC that Vinícius Júnior needs 'great emotional control' when provoked. Real Madrid also featured in a Liverpool story: former Anfield full-back Trent Alexander-Arnold told the BBC he still receives online abuse from Liverpool fans 14 months after leaving for Madrid.

Arsenal are reported by the BBC and Transfermarkt to be exploring a move for Juventus forward Kenan Yildiz, with Transfermarkt noting Juventus have responded to a reported €100m bid; neither outlet says a deal is close. The BBC and Transfermarkt separately report Arsenal have also made an approach for Victor Osimhen. Liverpool host Newcastle today under new head coach Andoni Iraola, entering the fixture without Mohamed Salah for the first time in a decade, per the BBC.
