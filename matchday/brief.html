Liverpool draw again as Man City's Maresca era takes shape

Liverpool were held 0-0 by newly promoted Fulham at Anfield, with Sky Sports, the Guardian and Opta Analyst all filing separate, independent reports on the same result. It was Liverpool's third draw in four Premier League games under new head coach Andoni Iraola, with both Opta and the Guardian describing a stuttering start to his tenure. Fulham, per Sky Sports, picked up their first point of the season.

Separately, BBC Sport and the Guardian both covered Manchester City's rebuild under new manager Enzo Maresca following Pep Guardiola's departure, two genuinely independent newsrooms rather than a repeated leak. Captain Ruben Dias told the BBC City's summer signings are "desperate to win" and called Maresca "the perfect person" for the job, while the Guardian's own data analysis found an unusually high rate of squad turnover compared with the Guardiola era.

Everything else touching this group is unconfirmed. BBC's gossip column, its only source, says Chelsea and Real Madrid are keen on Arsenal midfielder Martin Zubimendi, who may look to leave in January, and separately that Real Madrid's Endrick is drawing interest from Italian clubs. Transfermarkt alone reports Arsenal have completed the signing of Georgian teenager Andria Bartishvili; no other outlet in the feed corroborates it.
