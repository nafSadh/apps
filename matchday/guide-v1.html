League vs Cup

competition structures

Domestic leagues

EPL · La Liga · Serie A · Ligue 1 (Bundesliga: 34)

Double round-robin — every club plays every other home and away, 38 games (34 in Germany's 18-team league). Most points wins the title outright; no playoff.

Domestic cups

FA Cup · Copa del Rey · DFB-Pokal

Single-elimination knockouts with open draws across all divisions of the national pyramid — lower-league clubs can draw the giants.

Secondary cup

Carabao Cup (England)

England-only second knockout cup. Top-tier clubs typically rotate squads here and give minutes to young academy prospects.

Reading a Match

match metrics

Expected goals

xG

Probability that each shot scores, given its position and situation, summed per team. Measures chance quality — separates creating good chances from finishing them.

Post-shot xG

PSxG

Re-scores each on-target shot after it is struck, factoring placement and power. PSxG faced minus goals conceded measures a goalkeeper's shot-stopping.

Pressing intensity

PPDA

Opponent passes allowed per defensive action during their build-up. Lower numbers mean a more aggressive press; rising numbers late can signal fatigue.

Field tilt

final-third share

Each team's share of possession played in the attacking third. Captures territorial dominance that raw possession percentage hides.

Goal timing

goals by time block

Goal frequency per 15-minute window. Late-window spikes track fatigue, substitutions, and game-state pushes such as stoppage-time surges.

Forecasts

10,000-run simulation

Tournament outcomes modeled by simulating the remaining draw thousands of times from Elo ratings, UEFA coefficients and xG trends — the method behind the WC2026 final report.
