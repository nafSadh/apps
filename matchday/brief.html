Man City complete Bouaddi signing, chase Fernández

Manchester City completed the signing of 18-year-old Morocco midfielder Ayyoub Bouaddi from Lille, with BBC Sport and Sky both confirming a five-year deal reported at £86m (Transfermarkt puts the fee near €100m and calls it a record for an under-18 player). City are separately pursuing Chelsea's Enzo Fernández: BBC Sport values him around £120m, while Transfermarkt says City are preparing what would be a Premier League record bid. Sky reports City are considering an offer. Chelsea have not confirmed a deal.

Arsenal's interest in Atlético Madrid forward Julián Álvarez is the summer's most-covered story involving the club, reported by Sky, Transfermarkt and FourFourTwo. Accounts differ on its shape: Sky frames it as Álvarez choosing between staying at Atlético or joining Arsenal, while Transfermarkt and a separate Sky report both say Barcelona are also pursuing him, with Barcelona said to be asking Atlético to accept an offer. FourFourTwo, citing a single report, says Arsenal have begun talks. None of this is confirmed as a deal.

Liverpool's interest in Crystal Palace winger Ismaila Sarr is corroborated by both Sky and Transfermarkt: Sky reports an approach, and Transfermarkt says Liverpool submitted a formal offer after two rejected bids for a Galatasaray target. Separately, Transfermarkt alone reports Liverpool in talks with PSG over Bradley Barcola, and FourFourTwo alone reports Liverpool may sell another midfielder following Curtis Jones's departure. Neither of those single-sourced reports is confirmed.
