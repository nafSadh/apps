Arsenal Six Clear, City Beaten; Real Madrid, PSG Eye Suzuki

Arsenal's win over Hull is the day's most solid story: Sky Sports, BBC Sport and the Guardian all separately reported Martin Odegaard's goal secured a 1-0 win, taking Arsenal six points clear at the top of the Premier League. That is three independent newsrooms agreeing on a result, not a rumour needing hedging, which makes it the clearest settled item in today's feed for the six clubs tracked here.

Elsewhere the Premier League and Bundesliga were less kind. Manchester City lost 2-0 at Nottingham Forest, with Sky's own report calling City's performance sloppy and the Guardian covering the match separately in its live blog. Bayern Munich beat Mainz 2-1, per the Guardian, though the win was overshadowed by goalkeeper Manuel Neuer, who became the club's oldest-ever player that day but also made a mistake and picked up an injury.

On transfers, Real Madrid and PSG's interest in Aston Villa goalkeeper Zion Suzuki appeared in BBC's gossip column on Saturday and, separately, in a Transfermarkt report on Sunday -- genuine cross-outlet reporting, though still unconfirmed interest, not a deal. FourFourTwo also linked Real Madrid and PSG, unconfirmed elsewhere, to an unnamed new Aston Villa signing. Separately, Transfermarkt reported Bayern have confirmed a new contract for Harry Kane, and BBC's gossip column said Liverpool winger Trey Nyoni is set to stay this window.
