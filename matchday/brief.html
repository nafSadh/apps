Gabriel confirms Man Utd exit as Real Madrid move looms

Manchester United academy forward JJ Gabriel has confirmed he is leaving the club, posting a farewell message on social media. That departure is settled and reported independently by Sky Sports, BBC Sport and the Guardian. Where he goes next is less certain: all three describe a move to Real Madrid as close or expected, but Real Madrid have not confirmed anything themselves, so the destination remains reported rather than official.

Arsenal produced the day's most concrete transfer news. Sky Sports reports Declan Rice has verbally agreed a new long-term contract, a single-source claim with no independent corroboration yet. Separately, Sky Sports and FourFourTwo both report Arsenal are closing in on new deals for David Raya, Jurrien Timber and Piero Hincapie, with Raya's contract described by both as close to being finalised. None of the three are signed, confirmed deals.

Elsewhere, Manchester City's Josko Gvardiol is drawing transfer interest per FourFourTwo and Transfermarkt, though the two reports point in different directions: one has Manchester United poised to trigger a move, the other links Gvardiol to Barcelona. At Liverpool, Transfermarkt and FourFourTwo both noted Florian Wirtz excelling for Germany despite a difficult run at club level, with no transfer angle attached to either report.
