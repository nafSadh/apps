Arsenal's Guimaraes reports conflict; Liverpool flagged short

Arsenal's pursuit of Newcastle's Bruno Guimaraes is the closest thing to a running story in today's feed, and outlets disagree on where it stands: BBC Gossip said on July 31 the two clubs had agreed a fee, Sky reported August 3 that Newcastle had rejected Arsenal's opening bid outright, and BBC's gossip column returned on August 4 to describe Arsenal as having merely 'opened talks' for Guimaraes. None of those versions is confirmed elsewhere.

Sky also reports Liverpool's squad 'lacks depth' three weeks before the Premier League opener, with manager Andoni Iraola short in several positions and no further signings reported; a same-brand Sky Sports piece ran the identical framing, which is not independent confirmation. The same pattern holds for Fenerbahce's reported interest in Marcus Rashford, which BBC Gossip and BBC Sport both carried - one newsroom, not two.

At Real Madrid, Sky reports Vinicius Jr has 'broken his silence' after returning for pre-season training, which reads as the Arsenal interest floated in late July cooling rather than advancing; that earlier link was itself never confirmed. Sky's live blog also lists Manchester City as looking at a Marseille goalkeeper as a possible Trafford replacement, on a single unconfirmed line.
