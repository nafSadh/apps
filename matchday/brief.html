City's Bouaddi deal done, Fernandez chase continues

Manchester City completed the signing of 18-year-old Moroccan midfielder Ayyoub Bouaddi from Lille, for a fee reported as roughly £86m by Sky Sports and BBC Sport and around €100m by Transfermarkt — all three outlets independently confirmed the deal is done. City also lost a forward: Omar Marmoush joined Tottenham on loan, with an obligation to make it permanent for £60m next summer, reported separately by BBC Sport and Sky Sports.

Two pursuits remain unresolved. Manchester City are reported by BBC Sport, Sky Sports and Transfermarkt to be preparing a bid for Chelsea's Enzo Fernández — Transfermarkt called it a potential Premier League-record offer — though Chelsea's manager only gave an update, per Sky Sports, without confirming a sale. Arsenal's chase of Atlético Madrid's Julian Alvarez drew coverage from the same three outlets plus FourFourTwo, but accounts diverge: Sky Sports has Atlético blocking a Barcelona move, Transfermarkt says Alvarez still wants Barcelona, and FourFourTwo reports Arsenal in 'breakthrough' talks. Neither deal is done.

Liverpool have moved for Crystal Palace winger Ismaïla Sarr, with Sky Sports reporting an approach and Transfermarkt reporting an offer submitted, after stalled talks over Brighton's Yankuba Minteh, per Sky Sports. Earlier in the day, Palace manager Pierre Sage said he expected both Sarr and Jean-Philippe Mateta to stay at the club. Separately, Transfermarkt reported — in the only account of it — that Liverpool are in talks with Paris Saint-Germain over winger Bradley Barcola.
