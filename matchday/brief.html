Real Madrid win Clasico; Liverpool ease past Brighton

Real Madrid beat Barcelona 2-1 at Camp Nou in the first Clasico since Jose Mourinho's return as manager, with Kylian Mbappe scoring twice, the second a late winner. The Guardian, Opta Analyst and Transfermarkt each covered the result independently. Transfermarkt noted the goal put Mbappe into Real Madrid's all-time 100-goal club, with Cristiano Ronaldo now ranked second on that list. Opta Analyst's recap says the win puts Madrid back into the La Liga title race.

Liverpool beat Brighton 2-0 at Anfield, with Alexander Isak scoring again, per Opta Analyst's match report. Sky Transfers separately reported an injury update on Wirtz, saying the playmaker has responded to critics, though no further medical detail was given. Sky Transfers' own Hits and Misses column described Liverpool as having clicked into gear following the result. Both the match report and the injury note are confirmed club updates, not transfer speculation.

On the transfer side, BBC Gossip reports three Premier League clubs are monitoring Bayern Munich's Lennart Karl, an early rumour not yet confirmed elsewhere. Transfermarkt's live blog links Real Madrid with Rayan Cherki while reporting PSG have joined the race for JJ Gabriel; both are single-source and unconfirmed. FourFourTwo reports Arsenal have been keen on Athletic Bilbao's Nico Williams in the past, but he says he does not want to leave the club.
