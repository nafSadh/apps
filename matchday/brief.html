Rodri's Barcelona move confirmed; Arsenal agree Konsa deal

Rodri has completed his move from Manchester City to Barcelona. Sky Sports reports the transfer as done for £65.4m, a figure Transfermarkt independently corroborates at €76.5m. The Guardian quotes Jose Mourinho confirming Real Madrid also made an offer for the Spain midfielder and discussed it with him directly, but he chose Barcelona instead. Sky reports City are now looking for a replacement.

Arsenal have agreed a deal for Aston Villa's Ezri Konsa. Sky Sports and BBC Sport both put the fee at just over £50m, and Transfermarkt separately reports the move, linking it to cover for an injured William Saliba. All three describe the deal as agreed rather than completed; none reports it as officially announced.

Liverpool's Curtis Jones is reported close to joining Inter Milan. Sky Sports calls the talks advanced but unresolved, while Transfermarkt separately covers the same move, framing it as one of the club's most expensive academy sales without calling it final. Neither club has confirmed a transfer, so this remains reported rather than settled.
