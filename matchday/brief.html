Arsenal's Guimarães deal done, Man City closes in on Bouaddi

Arsenal have signed Bruno Guimarães from Newcastle, with BBC Sport, the Guardian, Opta Analyst and FourFourTwo all covering the move as completed. The Guardian puts the fee at £75m and says the Brazilian is expected to add creativity to an attack that struggled last season to break down low blocks; FourFourTwo reports he had wanted the move since January. It is the clearest finished deal of the day among the six clubs tracked here.

Manchester City's reported move for Ayyoub Bouaddi is the most-covered story among the six clubs: BBC Gossip, Sky, the Guardian and FourFourTwo all have it, though BBC and Sky each cite a sister outlet (BBC Sport, Sky Sports) rather than a second newsroom. The Guardian ties an £85m fee to Rodri's expected departure for Barcelona, who have reportedly raised their bid for him; FourFourTwo instead puts City's cost at £60m. Neither the Bouaddi signing nor the Rodri exit is confirmed as done.

Liverpool have confirmed a loan-with-option-to-buy deal for Barcelona defender Ronald Araujo, per Sky Sports — the one uncontested done deal in today's feed. At Real Madrid, Opta Analyst reports the club has completed a reported €140m signing of Yan Diomande, calling him the most expensive player in the club's history, while BBC Sport separately reports Vinicius Júnior has chosen to stay at the Bernabéu rather than leave.
