Bayern stunned at Lille as Real Madrid, Liverpool win

Wednesday's Champions League round produced results for all six clubs this brief tracks, reported independently by BBC Sport and the Guardian. Bayern Munich suffered their first European defeat of the season, beaten 2-1 at Lille after an Aleksander Pavlovic own goal turned the game. Real Madrid came from behind to beat PSV, with Jude Bellingham scoring off the bench to keep their 100% Champions League record intact.

Liverpool beat Club Brugge 3-0, Alexander Isak scoring twice and Florian Wirtz once, bouncing back from their earlier Champions League loss to Fenerbahce, per BBC Sport and the Guardian. Arsenal were held to a draw by Borussia Dortmund after leading, and Manchester City were pegged back by Napoli despite Rayan Cherki's late equaliser - City have won just one of their last seven games in all competitions, per BBC Sport. Paris St-Germain's unbeaten European start ended in a 2-2 draw at Roma, Donyell Malen scoring against them for a fourth straight match.

On the transfer side, UK newspapers report Liverpool have agreed a deal for Senegalese teenager Ndiaye, per Sky Sports's gossip column - not yet confirmed by the club. Paris Saint-Germain's interest in winger Estevao appears in both the BBC's gossip column and Transfermarkt's transfer blog, which also reports Real Madrid are in talks involving Jude Bellingham; none of this is confirmed, and all of it should be read as reported interest rather than a done deal.
