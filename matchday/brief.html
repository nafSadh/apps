Man City's guilty verdict is this week's lead story

Manchester City's guilty verdict is the week's best-corroborated story, covered independently by BBC Sport, Sky Sports and the Guardian. The outlets differ on scope: BBC Sport says City were found guilty of the majority of their 115 Premier League financial charges, while Sky Sports reports guilt on all counts. Manager Enzo Maresca said the 'feeling is fantastic' among the squad, and Erling Haaland urged fans to 'stick together.' The Guardian says chairman Khaldoon al-Mubarak's next move may be a sale of the club. BBC Sport and Sky Sports both separately report Pep Guardiola plans to attend City's Champions League match against PSG next week.

Elsewhere the moves are mostly unconfirmed. Sky Sports' live blog reports Declan Rice is close to a new Arsenal deal, and a separate BBC gossip roundup says Martin Odegaard is set to sign a new Arsenal contract too, though only BBC's own output carries that claim. Transfermarkt's wire has Kylian Mbappé unhappy at Real Madrid while PSG weigh a move for Rayan Cherki, and BBC's gossip column reports Bayern Munich closing in on a new deal for Harry Kane. None of these is corroborated by a second outlet.

Liverpool host Manchester City this weekend. Sky Sports credits right-back Ronald Araujo's form for a defense that has conceded only twice in its last five matches, and FourFourTwo's review of Andoni Iraola's start puts Liverpool at four wins and three draws from seven games. Opta Analyst notes City's historically poor record at Anfield, a fixture now arriving amid the club's guilty verdict.
