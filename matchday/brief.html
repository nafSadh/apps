Man City move clear as Real Madrid, Arsenal lose

Manchester City moved clear atop the Premier League with a 5-3 win over Sunderland; Erling Haaland became the first player to score against all 25 clubs he has faced in the competition, per Opta Analyst. Real Madrid lost the Madrid derby 2-1 at Atlético, with Dean Huijsen sent off and Diego Simeone ending his losing run against Jose Mourinho. Arsenal were beaten at Brighton — the Guardian called it a "battering" — while Liverpool beat Bournemouth 1-0 on an Isak goal, extending their unbeaten start as Bournemouth's winless run continued.

Everything else is single-source and unconfirmed. Transfermarkt reports Arsenal are tracking Maximilian Beier while Liverpool are watching Lamine Camara. FourFourTwo separately reports a Liverpool offer for a defensive midfielder and says Arsenal have been "given the green light" for a January forward move, without naming a target. None of these reports were corroborated by a second outlet in today's feed.

On the punditry side, Sky's Jamie Carragher called Florian Wirtz's display at Bournemouth one of the worst of his Liverpool career and said he would not select him to face Manchester City next. Sky Sports pundit Paul Merson argued City have "no way" of winning the title if their current form continues — an opinion at odds with City's result over Sunderland the same weekend.
