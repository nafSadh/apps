02 · Shift

How every county moved

Each arrow is a county. It points left when the county moved toward the Democrat and right when it moved toward the Republican, and its length is the change in margin. Thicker arrows are bigger counties.

→

Margin is the two-party gap (Republican share minus Democratic share of the two-party vote). Third-party votes, like Perot's in 1992 and 1996, are left out of the margin, so a county can shift without either party gaining votes.
