Kompany returns to Bayern training after collapse

Bayern Munich manager Vincent Kompany was back at the club's training base this week, his first appearance there since he collapsed, as reported independently by both Sky Sports and BBC Sport. Two separate newsrooms carrying the same account, rather than one outlet's report being echoed elsewhere, makes this the best-attested story in today's run. Neither report cites a club statement on the underlying cause, so his health status beyond "back at training" is not confirmed.

At Liverpool, player futures were discussed without new deals materializing. Alisson told reporters, per Sky Sports, that he wants to stay at Anfield beyond this season, when his current contract expires; Virgil van Dijk is separately described by Sky as "not panicking" about his own talks. Both are single-source accounts of player sentiment, not club confirmations. Sky and FourFourTwo also each reported, on separate days, that Liverpool are exploring a move for Brighton goalkeeper Bart Verbruggen, though neither cites a club source.

Everything else involving the six clubs stayed at rumour level. BBC's gossip column, uncorroborated elsewhere, linked Atletico Madrid with a move for Arsenal midfielder Martin Zubimendi, and earlier in the week linked Paris Saint-Germain and Real Madrid with Aston Villa goalkeeper Zion Suzuki. FourFourTwo ran two further Arsenal items, on unnamed incoming deals and a possible January exit after friction with Mikel Arteta, but gave no names or specifics in either case.
