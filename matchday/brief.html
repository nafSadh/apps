Rice's new Arsenal deal: reported, not signed

Arsenal provide the day's most concrete transfer story: Sky Sports News reports that Declan Rice has verbally agreed terms on a new long-term contract at Arsenal. The item lists 'Sky Sports' as a second source, but that is the same newsroom as Sky Sports News, not independent confirmation — nothing from a different outlet corroborates the deal. Until Arsenal confirm it, the Rice contract should be read as reported, not done.

Manchester City and Real Madrid both surface in single-source reports with no corroboration. Both are speculative, from the same gossip column, and not evidence of one deal advancing.

Liverpool's only mention in today's feed is a FourFourTwo report that the club will not enter the January or next-summer market to replace Virgil van Dijk — again a single-outlet claim, not a confirmed plan.
