Real Madrid Lead Race for Man Utd Wonderkid Gabriel

Real Madrid are reported to be leading the race to sign Manchester United's 15-year-old forward JJ Gabriel, according to both Sky Sports and the Guardian. Bayern Munich and Barcelona are also named as admirers, with Paris Saint-Germain and unnamed Premier League clubs said to be interested. United are said to be hoping to convince Gabriel to stay. None of this is confirmed; it remains a reported transfer target situation, not a completed deal.

Elsewhere, the Carabao Cup's fourth-round draw is confirmed: Liverpool will host Chelsea, while Premier League champions Arsenal have drawn the competition's lowest-ranked remaining side, Fleetwood Town, per Sky Sports. Arsenal have also given Kai Havertz rest time during the international break, per the same outlet. Manchester City, meanwhile, made ten changes for their third-round tie against Norwich City, handing a debut to Allan, as covered live by Sky Sports and the Guardian.
