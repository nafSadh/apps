Arsenal perfect through three as Bayern draw, PSG fall

Arsenal beat Chelsea 2-1, coming from behind to extend their perfect start to three wins from three league games. It's the best-covered story touching any of the six tracked clubs today, with independent accounts from the Guardian (match report, podcast, and a stadium-security feature), Opta Analyst, and FourFourTwo. Martin Odegaard was singled out as the standout performer, and the Guardian noted Chelsea's midfield struggled without the injured Moises Caicedo.

Bayern were held to a goalless draw away to Schalke, in a game the Guardian framed as a goalkeeping duel: Manuel Neuer made his return in the Bayern goal, but it was Schalke's Loris Karius who denied Harry Kane and the visitors. PSG lost 2-1 at home to Monaco in Ligue 1; the Guardian's column notes Monaco now sit top of the table but frames their position as more complicated than the scoreline alone suggests.

Real Madrid's Kylian Mbappe said he believes this could be his year to win the Ballon d'Or, ahead of Madrid's Champions League match against Inter on Tuesday. Manchester City are reported by Sky Sports to be without Jeremy Doku and Nico O'Reilly for their trip to Porto, unconfirmed by City themselves. Liverpool are searching for a new sporting director after Richard Hughes' departure, per the BBC, while a separate FourFourTwo report says the club's interest in Michael Olise has cooled - a claim not corroborated elsewhere in today's feed.
