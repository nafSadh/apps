A quiet news day, but Arsenal and Real Madrid surface in gossip

Little of substance moved for the six clubs on this cycle. The most notable line comes from the BBC's Friday gossip column, which reports Arsenal are eyeing Bournemouth youngster Eli Junior Kroupi while Real Madrid are considering a move for Everton's Jarrad Branthwaite. The same column links Chelsea with Nico Williams. All of this is single-sourced gossip; no other outlet in today's feed corroborates either the Arsenal or the Real Madrid link.

Otherwise it was fixtures rather than transfers. Real Madrid travel to Atlético on Sunday for La Liga's first Madrid derby of the season, and Paris Saint-Germain visit Marseille the same day for Le Classique, coming off their first Ligue 1 win of the campaign under Luis Enrique, both per Opta Analyst previews. Bayern Munich, aiming to go top of the Bundesliga table under Vincent Kompany, face Union Berlin on Friday night, per FourFourTwo. None of these previews carried transfer or squad news.
