Webb admits VAR error in Man City's derby win over Utd

Howard Webb, English football's referees chief, said the VAR review of Sunday's Manchester derby had "tunnel vision": officials never checked whether Enzo Fernandez was offside before Erling Haaland's winning goal for Manchester City over Manchester United, and the result stands. Webb also backed Phil Foden's red card as correct, said Bruno Fernandes should have been booked for the "petulant" kick that sparked it, and called the accidental-handball law "a nonsense" needing reform. The Guardian, Sky Sports and BBC Sport all carried Webb's comments.

On the rumour mill, BBC's gossip column reported Bayern Munich are monitoring Liverpool's Florian Wirtz, a claim Transfermarkt repeated two days later; neither is confirmed, and Sky's Paul Merson separately said he is "worried" about Wirtz's and Bradley Barcola's form at Liverpool. BBC's gossip column also reported Real Madrid and Chelsea are interested in Arsenal's Martin Zubimendi, who may look to leave in January, and separately said Arsenal are monitoring Como midfielder Nico Paz. All of this is single-source gossip, not confirmed by any club.

In domestic cups, Liverpool hosted Tottenham and Arsenal played away at Ipswich in Carabao Cup third-round ties on Tuesday evening, per live coverage from the Guardian and Sky Sports; the feed carries no final score for either match. Liverpool went into their tie off a goalless draw with Fulham at the weekend, per the Guardian's live blog.
