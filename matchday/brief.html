City beat Arsenal in WSL; Premier League appeal filed

Manchester City beat Arsenal 4-2 in the Women's Super League on Sunday, coming from behind twice to win. Both Sky Sports and the Guardian covered the match, and the Guardian reports the result leaves City ten points clear of Arsenal, who sit low in the table with pressure mounting on manager Slegers, per FourFourTwo's preview. It is the most solidly confirmed story of the day involving the six clubs, since the scoreline itself is not in dispute.

Off the pitch, Sky Sports reports Manchester City have formally lodged their appeal against the independent commission's finding that they are guilty on all charges of breaching Premier League financial rules. Sky separately reports a 2015 statement from club sponsor Etihad Airways, submitted to the US government, has raised questions about City's appeal strategy. Both reports come from Sky alone in today's feed, with no second outlet corroborating either detail.

Everything else involving the six clubs stayed at rumour level today. Sky Sports' round-up of Sunday's papers has Arsenal leading the race for RB Leipzig winger Antonio Nusa. A BBC Gossip column reports Liverpool forward Federico Chiesa is drawing interest from a trio of Serie A clubs, and the same column suggests Erling Haaland is more likely to move to Barcelona than Arsenal if he leaves Manchester City. None of it is confirmed by a second outlet.
