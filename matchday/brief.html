Man City's Bouaddi Pursuit: Talks Confirmed, No Deal Yet

Manchester City's pursuit of Lille midfielder Ayyoub Bouaddi is the best-corroborated transfer story touching the six clubs tracked here. BBC first reported City closing in over a week ago; Sky Sports followed up with Lille head coach Davide Ancelotti confirming talks are ongoing, while saying he hopes the move collapses. That is two independent outlets confirming talks are live, not evidence of a completed transfer — Ancelotti's own comments suggest Lille has not agreed to sell.

Arsenal and Manchester City met in the Community Shield in Cardiff today; Sky's live coverage reported Arsenal scoring inside 24 seconds, though the feed carries no final result. Separately, Arsenal's interest in Jack Hinshelwood was reported by both a BBC gossip column and Transfermarkt, which also described Tottenham as close to an unrelated €180m double deal. Both are reported pursuits, not confirmed transfers.

Paris Saint-Germain have completed the signing of Ferran Torres from Barcelona, calling it a bargain deal, per Transfermarkt — the only outlet in today's feed carrying that claim. Real Madrid's season previews from the Guardian and Opta Analyst both center on new head coach José Mourinho after two trophyless seasons. Sky's Liverpool live blog reported Mohamed Salah made his debut for Trabzonspor, with no further detail on terms. Bayern had no notable news today.
