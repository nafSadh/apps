Kane's new Bayern deal confirmed; Liverpool's Euro run ends

Harry Kane has signed a contract extension with Bayern Munich, confirmed independently by Sky Sports, BBC Sport and Transfermarkt — today's most solidly sourced story. Sky's own headline cites the deal running to 2029, and Transfermarkt notes he took no pay rise despite winning the Ballon d'Or. BBC Sport frames it as likely ending any route back to the Premier League. This is a completed, official deal, not a rumour.

Tuesday and Wednesday's Champions League games moved several of the other five clubs. Per Transfermarkt, Real Madrid needed a late 3-2 win over AEK Athens with Jude Bellingham again influential, and PSG thrashed Villarreal 6-0 to stay unbeaten — the Guardian corroborates both sides "flexed their muscles." The Guardian also reports Manchester City and Arsenal each escaped with narrow wins. Liverpool fared worst: a 3-2 loss to Fenerbahce, their first Champions League defeat this season, also reported by Sky Sports, whose Jamie Carragher used it to question the club's recruitment.

Arsenal's situation off the pitch is murkier. FourFourTwo, citing a report, says the club have already blocked two potential January exits, and separately reports — via comments attributed to his father — that Mikel Merino could leave for a return to Spain. Neither claim is corroborated by another outlet in today's feed, so both remain speculative rather than settled.
