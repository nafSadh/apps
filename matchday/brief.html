Arsenal's White opens up on his England exile

Arsenal defender Ben White has spoken about his self-imposed exile from the England squad, in the best-corroborated story in today's feed: BBC Sport and the Guardian both reported his comments independently within an hour of each other. White said people do not know the "full story" behind his decision, that he has no complaints about being booed by England fans when he returned for the recent draw with Uruguay, and that he would make the same call again.

Manchester City are drawing scrutiny from several independent outlets after three straight defeats. The Guardian reports that manager Enzo Maresca says he will use the international break to reassess; Sky's Gary Neville has asked whether the club's Premier League charges are affecting performances; and FourFourTwo argues City still lack an answer to Rodri's continued absence. The three are separate pieces rather than one corroborated story, but together they point to a club under real pressure.

Elsewhere the reporting stays rumour-only and mostly single-sourced. BBC gossip pages have linked Real Madrid and Paris Saint-Germain with Aston Villa goalkeeper Zion Suzuki, and separately reported Bayern Munich in talks over Rio Ngumoha's camp and Barcelona interest in Bayern defender Jonathan Tah. Liverpool's goalkeeper situation cuts both ways: Alisson has told Sky he wants to stay beyond his expiring contract, while BBC gossip and a later FourFourTwo report have both named Brighton's Bart Verbruggen as a target, with neither outlet reporting that contact has been made.
