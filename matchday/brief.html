Liverpool complete £123m Barcola deal; City turn to Ndiaye

Liverpool have completed the signing of Bradley Barcola from Paris Saint-Germain for £123m, confirmed independently by Sky Sports, the Guardian and Transfermarkt. Sky Sports and Transfermarkt both describe it as the most expensive transfer of the summer window. The Guardian reports Barcola said winning the Premier League is 'one of my biggest dreams,' and noted Liverpool separately rejected an £80m Manchester City bid for winger Cody Gakpo over the weekend.

For Manchester City, one deal is done and two are still moving. The Guardian reports City have confirmed the signing of Allan for an initial £32.1m. Separately, the BBC reports City's £80m move for Gakpo is 'close to collapsing,' with City instead agreeing a £65m fee for Everton's Iliman Ndiaye — reported independently by both BBC Sport and Sky Sports, though neither describes it as complete. A Sky report of City opening talks with Chelsea over Enzo Fernandez remains single-sourced.

Arsenal are exploring a late move for Lyon winger Malick Fofana, reported independently by Sky Sports and Transfermarkt, though Transfermarkt's report questions whether Arsenal will meet his valuation — no bid is confirmed. There was no Bayern Munich or Real Madrid news of note in today's feed.
