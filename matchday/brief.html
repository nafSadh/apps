Arsenal open season with a rout; deals stay just talk

Arsenal opened their Premier League title defence with a 3-0 home win over promoted Coventry City, a result confirmed independently by both Sky Sports' match report and Opta Analyst's separate stats coverage. On Sky's panel, Gary Neville repeated his view that Arsenal still need a "world-class attacker" and pushed for a Julian Alvarez deal before the window shuts. The Guardian's Barney Ronay singled out Martin Ødegaard's passing as a sign of recovery from an injury-hit season.

Sky reports Atlético Madrid are now willing to sell Alvarez to Arsenal, but that account comes from Sky's own transfer desk and its sister property Sky Sports rather than a second, independent outlet. Separately, a reported Liverpool enquiry for Endrick and Arsenal interest in Victor Osimhen appeared in both a BBC gossip column and a later Transfermarkt roundup — two genuinely different outlets — though both describe enquiries and interest, not agreed deals.

At Manchester City, the search for a replacement for Rodri, who left for Barcelona per FourFourTwo's analysis, continues to generate reports rather than confirmation: a BBC gossip column says Enzo Fernández would cost City more than £120m, while Transfermarkt separately reports City are looking at Palmeiras winger Allan Elias as a Savinho replacement. Both are single-source at this stage, with no fee or deal confirmed for either player.
