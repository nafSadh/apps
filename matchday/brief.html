Man City guilty on most PL charges; Liverpool rehire Ward

Manchester City have been found guilty on the majority of the Premier League's 115 financial charges, according to both BBC and the Guardian, and the case now moves to an appeal stage. Chairman Khaldoon Al Mubarak said the club remains confident of proving its innocence, insisting "nothing has changed." BBC separately reports there is no appetite within the Premier League to strip City of past titles, though the threat of further sanctions on appeal remains open.

Liverpool have made one move official: Sky Sports and the Guardian both report the club has rehired Julian Ward as sporting director, replacing Richard Hughes, who left for Al-Hilal. Real Madrid's issues remain unresolved by contrast — BBC Sport's analysis says Jose Mourinho, back since July, still has plenty to fix for a La Liga title race. Separately, Sky Sports alone reports Arsenal and Liverpool are tracking Brentford's Kevin Schade, in his own contract talks — a single-outlet rumour, unconfirmed elsewhere in today's feed.
