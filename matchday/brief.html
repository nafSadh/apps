Liverpool, PSG agree Barcola deal worth up to £123m

Liverpool and Paris Saint-Germain have agreed a transfer for winger Bradley Barcola worth up to £123m, according to both Sky Sports and the Guardian — two separate newsrooms reporting the same deal. The Guardian says Liverpool have finalised the move and that Barcola is due for a medical this weekend; Sky Sports similarly reports the clubs have settled the transfer's structure. Neither club has made an official announcement, so the deal is advanced but not yet formally confirmed.

Arsenal's interest in Atlético Madrid's Julián Álvarez continues: Sky Sports reports Atlético have given him until Sunday to decide between staying and joining Arsenal, and BBC's gossip column has carried the same story most days this week — though that column is a single outlet's roundup, not independent confirmation. Separately, Real Madrid manager José Mourinho told the Guardian that PSG's Kylian Mbappé, not a Real Madrid or Spain player, deserves this year's Ballon d'Or; that is a stated opinion, not a transfer development.

Separately, Paris Saint-Germain appear set to lose academy forward Ibrahim Mbaye to Aston Villa. The Guardian and Transfermarkt both report an agreement has been reached, with Transfermarkt describing him as a 'wonderkid' and former Liverpool target. Neither PSG nor Villa has confirmed the move publicly, so — as with Barcola — call it agreed in principle rather than completed.
