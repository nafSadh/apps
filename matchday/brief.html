Arsenal stretch lead; City's winless run drags on

Arsenal moved six points clear at the top of the Premier League despite a 2-2 draw at Newcastle, rescued by a second-half Noni Madueke goal, as reported by BBC Sport, the Guardian and Sky Sports. The draw ended a four-game Premier League winning run in which Arsenal had not conceded, per Sky Sports. Both the Guardian and Sky Sports reported that goalkeeper David Raya was injured during the match, which Sky Sports pundit Jamie Redknapp called a 'massive blow.'

Manchester City's troubles continued as they were held 1-1 by Fulham at the Etihad, Elliot Anderson's volley merely ending a three-game losing run rather than starting a winning one; City have now gone four league games without a win, according to the Guardian, BBC Sport and Sky Sports. The Guardian also noted City are awaiting sanction after being found guilty of financial breaches, adding to the pressure around the club.

Elsewhere, Real Madrid confirmed Arda Güler has signed a contract extension through 2032, per Transfermarkt, which separately reported Madrid are still expected to trigger a buy-back clause for Como's Nico Paz next summer — unconfirmed by the club. The Guardian reported Harry Kane has vowed to return from his Bayern injury. BBC's gossip column reported Liverpool are leading Chelsea in the race for Monaco's Lamine Camara, though that remains unconfirmed transfer speculation.
