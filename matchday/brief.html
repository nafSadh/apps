Real Madrid's Rodri push is the best-sourced story

Manchester City's Rodri remains the most contested name in the market: Sky's transfer live blog and BBC's gossip column, reporting separately, both say Real Madrid are working to sign him, with BBC describing Madrid as confident. That is two independently run outlets agreeing, not one repeating itself — the strongest sourcing of any item in today's feed. Neither club has confirmed a deal, so this stays a reported target, not a signing.

Elsewhere, Sky alone reports Arsenal exploring a deal for Real Madrid's Vinicius Junior, with no second outlet backing it up. Liverpool's pursuit of Paris Saint-Germain forward Bradley Barcola has also only run through BBC's gossip column, first as contact between the clubs and later as Liverpool being confident of signing him. Both remain single-sourced rumours rather than confirmed moves.
