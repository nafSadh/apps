Real Madrid presses Tebas to resign over Goebbels jibe

Real Madrid have renewed their demand for La Liga president Javier Tebas to resign after he compared the club's conduct to Joseph Goebbels. Sky Sports, BBC Sport and the Guardian — three separate newsrooms, not one outlet republished twice — all reported the story, with the Guardian noting Tebas accused Madrid of using 'propaganda techniques' and the club calling for his 'immediate resignation' over 'constant attacks.' The demand itself is confirmed on the record; the underlying dispute remains unresolved.

Arsenal confirmed cuts of up to 60 roles in a club restructuring, which Mikel Arteta defended as necessary to 'achieve the success' the club wants — covered separately by BBC Sport and the Guardian. Manchester City's hopes of overturning their Premier League financial-rules guilty verdict look slim: a lawyer told BBC Sport the club faces 'substantial hurdles' on appeal, while Sky Sports reported manager Enzo Maresca saying the squad and fans feel 'more together' since the verdict landed.

Other lines were thinner. The Guardian examined why Florian Wirtz, a £116m signing, has yet to find his Leverkusen form at Liverpool. BBC's Friday gossip column claims, in one digest, that Harry Kane is close to a new Bayern Munich contract, Real Madrid's Arda Güler has agreed fresh terms, and Liverpool lead the chase for Bournemouth's Alex Scott; a Sky Sports live blog separately floated a Jude Bellingham new deal at Real Madrid. None of this is corroborated elsewhere, and PSG had no coverage in today's feed.
