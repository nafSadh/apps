Man City's flawless record meets a defence question

Manchester City's start is drawing scrutiny from two unrelated outlets. Opta Analyst's data review notes City are top of the Premier League with a perfect five wins from five games but have not been very convincing defensively. Sky Sports' writers separately ask whether City are genuine title contenders after the same five games, grouping the question with similar doubts about Manchester United and Tottenham. Two different organizations, same underlying point: City's results look flawless, their performances less so.

Arsenal had the day's most eye-catching headline: Sky Sports reports Mikel Arteta has agreed a new contract, two years after signing his last one in September 2024. That report comes from Sky alone, with no second outlet carrying it, so treat it as reported rather than independently confirmed. Also via Sky's papers round-up, Declan Rice is said to be prepared to commit long-term. Separately, FourFourTwo reports Arsenal are in talks over an "expensive" departure for Viktor Gyokeres, still without a Premier League start this season.

At Liverpool, Florian Wirtz's underwhelming form since his £116m move drew renewed attention, though from one analytical source rather than independent confirmation: the Guardian's piece on whether it is "early to write him off" carries an Opta Analyst byline, and Opta's own site separately revisited Jamie Carragher's on-air criticism of the player. Same data source, two venues, no verdict reached either way — a form debate, not a transfer or team-news story.
