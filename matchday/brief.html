Arsenal exits agreed; Gakpo's next club unclear

Arsenal have agreed a club-record £55.7m sale of Gabriel Martinelli to Al Hilal, with a deal in principle also struck for Gabriel Jesus to join Barcelona for £8.6m, according to Sky Sports. Transfermarkt's own reporting treats both exits as effectively done. The Sky Transfers and Sky Sports accounts come from the same newsroom, so Transfermarkt is the only independent outlet corroborating the story; neither move has been confirmed as completed by the clubs involved.

Cody Gakpo's future is contested. Transfermarkt reports he has agreed a 'shock switch' to Manchester City, a move it says could push City's summer transfer spend past €500m. Sky Transfers, however, reports that Liverpool captain Virgil van Dijk is pleading with Gakpo to stay at Anfield, suggesting the move is not settled. The two outlets do not agree on where things stand, and neither claim is confirmed by the clubs.
