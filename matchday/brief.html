Arsenal's Martinelli sale confirmed as window shuts

Arsenal have sold Gabriel Martinelli to Saudi club Al-Hilal for a club-record £60m, a done deal rather than a rumour: Sky Transfers, the Guardian and Transfermarkt all report it independently, and the Guardian says Arsenal have confirmed the fee. The Guardian notes the forward follows Gabriel Jesus out of the club, while Sky says his exit leaves only one player left from Mikel Arteta's first-ever squad. Separately, both BBC's gossip column and Transfermarkt report Arsenal still want Bournemouth striker Eli Junior Kroupi for January — unconfirmed, and for the next window rather than this one.

At Liverpool, head coach Andoni Iraola told the Guardian he was right not to throw 'crazy money' at short-term fixes this summer and said he is happy with the club's four current wingers. Sky Transfers notes Liverpool's net spend was actually higher than last summer's, over £200m, but argues the squad still looks imbalanced. Separately, Crystal Palace boss Pierre Sage said Ismaila Sarr is still recovering emotionally from a failed move to Liverpool, with a January approach not ruled out, per FourFourTwo.

Elsewhere: the Guardian reports Paris Saint-Germain have drawn both league games so far this season, with manager Luis Enrique saying he has faith in 'new energy' from the summer signings ahead of Friday's match against Monaco. The BBC reports Jude Bellingham is 'thriving' at Real Madrid under Jose Mourinho after his World Cup form. At Manchester City, FourFourTwo reports Enzo Fernandez has been given the number 17 shirt previously worn by Kevin De Bruyne, while BBC's gossip column reports unconfirmed interest from Sunderland in City winger Jack Grealish. No fresh Bayern news in today's feed.
