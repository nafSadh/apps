Liverpool edge nine-man Brentford; City's appeal in doubt

Liverpool's best-corroborated story today: a 2-1 comeback win at Brentford, reported independently by the Guardian and Opta Analyst, with Isak scoring the decisive goal as Brentford were reduced to nine men. BBC Sport's Match of the Day panel said Liverpool are still "too easy" to play against despite the result. The Guardian separately reports Liverpool will be without Szoboszlai for "some time"; no further detail on the issue was given.

Manchester City face "substantial hurdles" in their appeal against being found guilty of breaking the Premier League's financial rules, a lawyer told BBC Sport, which is the only outlet in today's feed on that story. Separately, per BBC Sport, City's men's team is unbeaten in seven league games this season, and Sky Sports reports City's women kept their perfect Women's Super League start with a 3-2 win over Brighton.

Elsewhere, unconfirmed items from BBC's gossip column - the feed's only source for each - say Harry Kane is close to a new Bayern Munich contract, Real Madrid's Arda Guler has agreed a new deal, and Jude Bellingham is "ready to commit" his future to Real Madrid. Arsenal's Viktor Gyokeres is reported to want to leave the club; BBC Sport ran the same line, but since both outlets are BBC properties, that is one newsroom, not independent confirmation. None of this is official.
