Liverpool's Hughes departure is the day's firmest story

Liverpool's sporting director Richard Hughes is leaving the club, with Sky, the Guardian and BBC Sport all reporting it independently — three separate newsrooms rather than one story reprinted. Hughes is expected to take a similar role at Saudi club Al-Hilal, per the Guardian.
