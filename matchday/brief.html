Arsenal's win at Anfield still the headline

Arsenal's 1-0 win at Liverpool on Sunday, discussed on Sky Sports by Paul Merson as proof of a "mark of champions", put the Gunners three points clear at the top of the Premier League. Ahead of Wednesday's Champions League trip to Slavia Prague, William Saliba is back in first-team training, reported independently by both Sky Sports and BBC Sport, leaving Mikel Arteta with a selection decision at centre-back.

Liverpool face Fenerbahce in the Champions League in Istanbul, chasing a fourth straight win in the competition; the Guardian reports manager Andoni Iraola wants sharper attacking play from his side beforehand, though he played down any contrast with their domestic form after Sunday's defeat at home to Arsenal. Arsenal, by contrast, travel to Slavia Prague as Opta's supercomputer makes them heavy favourites.

Elsewhere, Jude Bellingham became the fastest midfielder in Real Madrid's history to reach 50 goals, per Transfermarkt, while Opta's FotMob data rates teammate Kylian Mbappe the best-performing player in Europe for October. At Bayern Munich, Harry Kane, speaking after a 6-1 win over Magdeburg, told Transfermarkt his Ballon d'Or recognition could lift the wider Bundesliga's standing. At Manchester City, Phil Foden told Sky Sports he feels close to his best form again and is targeting a recall to the England squad.
